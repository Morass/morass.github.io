Bone Tankard — skeleton cup holder with femur staves & skull

A barrel-stave tankard built from bones: 24 randomized cartoon femurs as staves, a relief skull fused into the front, and a bone-chain handle. Drop in a glass or 330 ml can. Prints upright with NO supports. Fully parametric OpenSCAD.

Cup Holder Car — rolling toy coupe with a drink well in the roof

A chunky toy coupe whose roof is a real Ø73 mm drink well — decorative AND functional. The four wheels actually roll on captive snap-on axles. No glue, no supports, fully parametric OpenSCAD.

Cup Holder with Print-in-Place Chain Handle

A portable cup / can holder that hangs from a handbag-style chain handle — printed in place, no chain assembly. Skeleton cage cup fits cans and cups up to ~75 mm. Split jump-ring end links clip the chain to the cup horns. Drape it over a door handle, stroller bar, chair arm, or hook.

Tulip Cup Holder — clamp-on flower drink holder for your desk

A blooming tulip that clamps to the edge of your desk and cradles a can, cup or mug. Six overlapping petals form the cup and a leafy stem carries a printed thumbscrew C-clamp. Fully parametric — dial the clamp gap to ANY desk thickness and the screw auto-lengthens to match.

Lazy-Susan Drink Turntable — 4-cup, drop-in ball bearing

A spinning drinks caddy that keeps turning smoothly even loaded with full cups. The cup weight rides on a ring of drop-in steel balls (NOT printed) so friction stays low under load. Print two parts, drop in cheap 8 mm balls, set the top on — done. Four 90 mm cup wells fit mugs and pint glasses. Parametric OpenSCAD source included.
