Kitchen

Sink-Rim Sponge Tray — Hooks Over the Rim, Drains Into the Basin

Single-bay sponge tray that hooks over a kitchen sink rim and hangs inside the basin. Sized to a standard kitchen sponge (~95×60×25 mm) with ~5 mm slack on every side. Floor drain slots + lower-wall drains let water run straight back into the sink — sponge dries instead of sitting in a puddle. Prints in one piece, open-top up, no supports.
