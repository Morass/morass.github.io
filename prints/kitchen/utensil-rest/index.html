Kitchen

Utensil Rest — spoon / ladle / spatula rest with a deep drip bowl, anti-soak ribs and a raised handle saddle

A hob-side spoon rest that keeps the handle off the counter. The spoon head sits in a 8.5 mm-deep elliptical drip bowl, radial ribs hold it up out of its own drips, and a 30 mm saddle at the end cradles the handle in a U-groove. One piece, 132 x 88 x 30 mm, prints flat with no supports. Fully parametric OpenSCAD source included.
