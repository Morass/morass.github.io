Napkin Tray — Toblerone-Style Holder w/ Diamond Cross-Hatch

Upright napkin holder shaped like a Toblerone bar: flat rounded base + two isosceles-triangular side walls leaning in at the peak. Folded paper napkins (~8×16 cm) stack between the walls. Diamond cross-hatch engraved on every visible face — top of the base + both outer faces of each wall. Prints flat on bed, single piece, no supports.
