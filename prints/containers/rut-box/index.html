Rut Box — a keepsake box locked shut by two stags' antlers

A 108 x 62 x 84 mm hunting keepsake box with a 64 ml cavity. Four antler racks — two on the base, two on the lid — interleave when the box is closed, like two stags with their horns locked in the rut. A quarter turn sweeps the racks apart and releases a bayonet lock. No hardware, no supports, no glue.
