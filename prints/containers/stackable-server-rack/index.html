Desk & office

Stackable Server Rack — Tool-Free Mini-PC / NUC / SBC Shelf

A modular, tool-free rack for mini-PCs, NUCs, SBCs and other small servers. Each level is a honeycomb-vented floor on four fat corner posts; levels self-stack on cone pegs (no screws, no hardware) and four wide feet lift the whole tower off the desk for airflow. Print one floor per device, four feet once, stack as tall as you like. Parametric SCAD — resize for any box.
