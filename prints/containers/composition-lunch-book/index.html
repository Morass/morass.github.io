Composition Book Lunchbox — a snack caddy shaped like a school notebook

A 150 x 105 x 52 mm lunch/snack caddy that looks like a marbled composition notebook. Print-in-place-style pin hinge (the pin prints too), no hardware, no supports, no glue. Three parts, three colours, and the colour split costs about 1.4 g of purge for the whole set.
