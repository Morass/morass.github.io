Piggy Bank 🐷 — top coin slot + finger-twist bottom plug

A classic chubby piggy bank: drop coins through the top slot (takes any circulating coin up to Ø33 mm), empty it by unscrewing the knurled bottom plug with your fingers — no tool. The pig prints as ONE belly-down body (snout, ears, curly tail, feet all integral) plus a separate twist plug. Watertight, no supports. Parametric OpenSCAD source included.
