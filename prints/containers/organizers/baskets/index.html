Stackable Lattice Basket — 4 Sizes + Parametric SCAD

Cross-stackable lattice baskets with cone-peg corner posts — any basket stacks onto any other basket. Four ready-to-print sizes from 5×5×5 cm cube up to a 20×12×8 cm tray, plus an optional cone-foot leg accessory. One open short side per basket so you can reach contents while the stack stays put. Parametric SCAD included — override basket_l/basket_w/body_h to derive any size; corner-post + peg geometry is shared so derived sizes still cross-stack with the printed ones.
