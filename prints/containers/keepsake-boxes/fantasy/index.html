Library of Alexandria box — a magnetic box under the great dome

The Library of Alexandria as a box: a white-marble hall behind an arcade of pointed arches, a full-width stair, four turrets under gilded onion domes and a great ribbed dome on a chevroned drum. Everything above the cornice is the lid, and eight Ø5 × 3 mm magnets buried mid-print hold it shut. 118 × 96 × 113 mm outside, 90 × 60 × 45 mm inside. Six STLs, two prints, no supports, no glue.

Castle keep box — a fortified keep that opens at the wall-walk

A 112 mm square castle keep whose lid is the wall-walk: lift the battlements, four corner drums and their tiled conical roofs straight off and the keep is a 70 x 70 x 64 mm box. Two prints, no supports, no hardware, nothing to glue. The lid rests rather than latches, so it is a table box, not a travelling one. Multi-colour for the AMS, and it still reads as a castle in one filament.

Coffin Pencil Case — Magnetic Box with a Death's-Head Moth Lid

A toe-pincher coffin box for pens, markers and brushes. The lid is held by six pairs of hidden magnets buried mid-print, so no fastener is visible anywhere. Prints flat, no supports, one colour or two.

Elven Motif Keepsake Box — vesica plan, interlaced vine walls, two-colour leaf inlay

A pointed-oval trinket box where every ornament is the same shape at three scales: the box plan, the leaves woven into the wall braid, and the veined leaf inlaid in the lid. The wall vine is a genuine over-and-under interlace, not two sine waves. Two printed parts, no supports, no hardware, no glue — and the two-colour lid costs about 1 g of purge because all the ink sits in three layers.

Grimoire Box — a hinged spellbook with the magnets sealed inside

A leather-bound tome that opens on a real barrel hinge. Two sizes, any word you like on the cover, and four magnets that end up fully enclosed in the plastic - nothing visible in any face. Three printed parts, no supports, no screws, no glue.

Pirate treasure chest — print-in-place hinged trinket box, two colour

A 100 mm barrel-top pirate chest that comes off the bed as one working unit. The piano hinge prints in place, so there is no assembly, no pin to fit and no glue. Two-colour for the AMS: wood planks plus iron straps, banding, corner brackets, lock plate and hasp.

Potion of Holding — Twist-Lock Flask Organizer (2 sizes)

A fantasy potion flask that twist-locks apart into a wide-mouth bin with a 4-way divider — four sorted compartments for screws, bolts, beads, pills, or tiny treasure. A friction cork plugs the neck. Two sizes from the same design: a full-size desk organizer and a mini that fits inside a 2.7-inch gacha capsule.

Sugar Skull Box — Calavera Trinket Box (2 Parts, No Supports)

A Day-of-the-Dead calavera trinket box that splits at the brow — the cranium lifts off as a lid. Two printed parts, slip-fit lip, no supports, no hardware. 62 × 58 × 74 mm closed, ~37 cm³ inside. Optional AMS two-colour inlay.

Trojan Horse Box — rolling, openable treasure horse

A wooden Trojan-horse keepsake box on real rolling wheels, with a hinged belly compartment — open it to reveal what you smuggled inside. NOTE: the hidden compartment is TINY (31 × 15 × 13 mm) — ring / note / tooth sized, not a storage box.
