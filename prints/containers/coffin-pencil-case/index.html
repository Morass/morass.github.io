Coffin Pencil Case — Magnetic Box with a Death's-Head Moth Lid

A toe-pincher coffin box for pens, markers and brushes. The lid is held by six pairs of hidden magnets buried mid-print, so no fastener is visible anywhere. Prints flat, no supports, one colour or two.
