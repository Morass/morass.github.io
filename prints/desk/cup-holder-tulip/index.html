Tulip Cup Holder — clamp-on flower drink holder for your desk

A blooming tulip that clamps to the edge of your desk and cradles a can, cup or mug. Six overlapping petals form the cup and a leafy stem carries a printed thumbscrew C-clamp. Fully parametric — dial the clamp gap to ANY desk thickness and the screw auto-lengthens to match.
