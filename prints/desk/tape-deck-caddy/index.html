Desk & office

Retro Tape Deck Desk Caddy — pen cups, phone dock, pull-out cassette drawer + swappable spine labels

A 1980s stereo cassette deck that is actually a desk organiser. Two 52 mm-deep pen cups, a catch-all tray, a tilted phone cradle with a cable pass-through, and a pull-out 'cassette' drawer whose spine label is a card you thread in sideways and swap. 9 spare captions on one sheet — or letter your own straight from the parametric OpenSCAD source. 3 printed parts, no hardware, no glue, no supports.
