Desk-Edge Cable Clamp — Clip-On with Printable Thumbscrew

A C-clamp that grips the edge of any desk and carries a cable through a C-shaped snap-in hook. Held in place by a fully printable knurled thumbscrew (plastic-on-plastic thread, no metal hardware, no tools). Three-part print.

Desk Cable Rack — Pegs or Pole Variant (Parametric)

A simple desk-side cable rack to keep charging cables, headphones, and props off the floor. Two variants share the same base + back wall: a 6-peg version (each cable hooks over its own peg) and a single-pole version (cables drape over a full-length pole with a back-stop shelf). 200 × 150 × 100 mm. Prints flat on its back, no supports.

USB Drive Caddy — Hex-Grill Open-Front Cubby

N×M open-front cubby for low-profile USB-C 'disc' drives. Drives lay flat and slide in from the front; ~4 mm sticks out for pinch removal. Decorative hex perforation on top + sides. Ships pre-rotated — open face prints UP, back wall on the bed, zero supports.
