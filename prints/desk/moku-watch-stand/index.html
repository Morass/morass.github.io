Moku Cantilever Watch Stand — open 'C' bracket + parametric engraved clock (Base Rail module)

A cantilever 'C' watch stand for a chunky sport/dive watch that clips onto the Moku modular desk-system Base Rail. A soft, near-rectangular bracket with smoothly filleted joints: a back spine carries a top arm that cantilevers forward over an open gap, its rest face CANTED ~20° SIDE-TO-SIDE so the watch leans on a dynamic diagonal. The watch sits centred over the base (so a heavy steel watch won't tip it off the rail); the bracelet drapes over the rounded front nose and hangs free, its end on the front shelf. The tall SPINE FRONT face — the back wall of the C, in full view through the opening — carries an ENGRAVED CLOCK FACE with PARAMETRIC hands (set the time in the SCAD, default 10:10) ringed by gear teeth. Every edge is rounded (Japandi). Prints PRINT-ON-END so the cantilever needs no supports and the rail foot prints as crisp vertical channels. A remix of the Moku Base Rail by @SabreDesign / @Collecticraft.
