Bathroom

Toothpaste Tube Squeezer — Roller Key, Diamond-Knurl Grip

Single-piece roller key for toothpaste / cosmetic tubes. Slot the empty end of the tube through the slit, fold once, twist the body — the tube wraps around the bar and you crank every last gram out. Hang hole at top. Diamond-knurl engraving on both faces for grip.
