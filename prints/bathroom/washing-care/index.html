Soap Dish — Wooden-Slat Cradle, Parametric Wood-Grain

Single-piece slatted soap dish that cradles oval and brick bars without rolling. Five saddle planks carry an oval bar; two flat fenders at each end keep larger blocks from migrating off. Drainage runs straight through the slat gaps onto the counter (or into a tray). Parametric wood-grain grooves on the slat sides — prints best in wood-fiber PLA for a real wooden look.

Toothpaste Tube Squeezer — Roller Key, Diamond-Knurl Grip

Single-piece roller key for toothpaste / cosmetic tubes. Slot the empty end of the tube through the slit, fold once, twist the body — the tube wraps around the bar and you crank every last gram out. Hang hole at top. Diamond-knurl engraving on both faces for grip.
