Bathroom

Tenshu Makeup Organizer — Japanese castle keep, 3 lift-off storeys

A Japanese castle keep (tenshu) that sorts makeup. Every roof is the lid of the storey under it: the base holds four compacts on edge in a lift-out comb plus a polish bay, the second storey has three coved lanes for a mascara, lipsticks and pencils lying down, the third a tweezer lane and two cells for lashes, pins and rings. Every pocket is a hull of four spheres, so the floor meets the wall on a 4 mm cove and nothing is a square corner or a blind hole: lift, lift, lift, tip, rinse. Five parts, no supports, no glue, no hardware — each lid seats on a 2.4 mm ring so the stack does not rock, and magnets are an option, not a need. Ships as one-colour STLs and, for a multi-filament printer, as one three-colour STL per part that Split to Parts divides into exactly three: cream plaster, dark timber, slate stone-and-tile. A five-colour split-to-parts STL per part ships too, if you have the slots for a separate plinth grey and gold shachihoko.
