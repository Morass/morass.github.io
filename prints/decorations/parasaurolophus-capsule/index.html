Parasaurolophus Skull Capsule — clamshell trinket / pill box

A detailed Parasaurolophus dinosaur skull that splits at the mouth and swings open on a hidden snap-together hinge, revealing a small interior pocket — a keepsake capsule for a pill, a tiny charm, a ring, or a rolled note. Two printed parts, no glue, no hardware. Printed in PETG.
