Deathly Hallows Fridge Magnet

The three Deathly Hallows as a slim fridge magnet — Cloak triangle, Resurrection-Stone circle, Elder-Wand line. Three Ø5×3 mm magnets are sealed invisibly inside the rounded corners during a single print pause: no glue, no exposed metal, either face sticks to the fridge.

Weather Dial Fridge Magnet — spin it to today's weather

A fridge magnet with a live front. Thumb the knurled rim through the side notch and the dial clicks into four detented positions — sun, rain, cloud, snow — showing one icon at a time through the porthole. The three magnets are sealed inside solid plastic on both faces: no glue, no exposed metal against the door, nothing to fall out. They go in during the print, at a single pause. The dial ships as five files sharing one coordinate frame, so it prints in five colours with nothing to group by hand. Prints supportless. Parametric OpenSCAD source included.

Wizard Figurine — robed mage with a magnet hand

A robed wizard figurine with a pointed wide-brim hat, long beard and one hand raised in greeting. The raised hand has a recessed slot for a small disc magnet, so he can hold a metal wand, pin or trinket. It is NOT a fridge magnet — the figure will not stick to a fridge, and the little magnet is weak, so think of it as a charming desk or shelf wizard with a handy magnetic hand. Ships as an STL plus a ready-to-slice Bambu .3mf.
