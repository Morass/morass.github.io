Hollow-Face Vase Brooch — the vase is a hole, and it follows you

A magnetic brooch, 47 x 55 mm. The vase is not raised — it is the exact mould of a vase, sunk 5.2 mm into the plaque. Your eye refuses to read it as hollow, so it bulges out instead, and appears to turn and follow whoever walks past. Nothing moves; the viewer does. Two printed pieces, four colours in one 0.6 mm band, sealed magnets, no pin.
