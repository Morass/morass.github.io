Strigoi bust — Romanian folklore vampire, one part, no supports

A strigoi — the Romanian-folklore vampire — as a small shelf bust: gaunt skull, deep-set eyes under a heavy brow, hooked nose, ears swept back to points, jaw hanging open on two long canines, and a folk-embroidered collar of cross-stitch diamonds that is the Carpathian tell separating a strigoi from a generic Dracula. 70.7 x 42.0 x 49.0 mm, one part, one colour, prints flat on its plinth with no supports, no glue and no hardware.
