Tengwar Wall Clock — Elvish Duodecimal Numerals

A Ø200 mm round wall clock with twelve authentic Tengwar duodecimal hour numerals and elvish leaf-blade hands. A real clock, not a flat disc: an integrated standoff back wall hides the quartz movement and a rear deep-channel keyhole hangs it on a single ordinary wall screw. Numerals + ticks can be paint-filled or printed as second/third AMS colours. Prints face-down, no supports.
