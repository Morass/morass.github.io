Yggdrasil Buttons — Norse world-tree sewing buttons, 15 / 20 / 25 mm, shank or 2-hole

Real, wearable sewing buttons with the Norse world-tree on the face. Five sizes/styles: Ø15, 20, 25 mm with a hidden thread tunnel in the underside (the face stays whole — no holes bored through the tree), plus classic 2-hole versions at Ø15 and 20. Each ships a named-solid 2-colour companion for AMS. Prints flat, face up, no supports, no brim. Fully parametric: any diameter renders with the motif's stroke widths automatically held at or above one nozzle width.
