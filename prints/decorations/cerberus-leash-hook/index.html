Cerberus leash hook — three-headed hellhound wall plaque with hidden magnets

A three-headed hellhound wall plaque where each open jaw is a hook. Five magnets sit fully sealed inside the body, dropped in at a print pause, so the back face is one unbroken plane with no bores, no glue and nothing to fall out.
