Winged Sandal Earrings — Hermes' talaria, flat two-colour pair

The winged sandal of Hermes (talaria, the 'Mercury foot') as a flat two-colour dangle earring — sole, up-curled toe and an ankle strap that arcs up and becomes the bail, with a feathered heel wing in a second colour. Both colours tile ONE flat plate at the same height, so they print flush in the same layers with no step, no overlap and no supports. ~35 mm tall, 2.2 mm thick, about 2 g for the pair. Mirrored L+R so the toes point outward when worn.
