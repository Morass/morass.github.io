Half-Amphora Brooch — a real amphora, full of flowers, cut in half

A magnetic brooch, 44 x 58 mm. Not an illusion and not a flat silhouette: a proper amphora is turned as a body of revolution, given two handles and a spray of flowers, and then cut in half on the plane of the brooch's back. What is left is 6 mm of true 3D relief with a flat back. The halving is also the print trick — a solid of revolution cut on its axis plane has zero overhang anywhere, so it prints face up with no supports. Two printed pieces, four colours, sealed magnets, no pin.
