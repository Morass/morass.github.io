Great White Shark Earring Charm — Bites the Ring

A realistic sleek great-white shark charm that bites a jump-ring held in its mouth. The ring is the hang point, so it wears nose-up / tail-down. About 37 x 66 x 25 mm. NOTE: the thin fins are very brittle — handle gently and print with supports.
