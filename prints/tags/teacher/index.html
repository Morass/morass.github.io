Classroom Desk Name Plate — slide-in letter tiles, one alphabet sheet spells the whole class

A school desk name plate whose name is not baked into the STL. One dovetail track, one tile per letter, threaded in from the open end. Print one alphabet sheet for the whole class and one plate per desk — re-spell it when the seating plan changes instead of re-slicing a custom model for every child. 41 tiles (A-Z plus Czech accented capitals), blanks that centre the name, an end stop that locks the row, and a pair of brackets that stand the plate up as a tent. Every letter also ships as its own file, plain and two-colour. No hardware, no glue, no supports.

Around the home / Hooks

Bag Hook for a Desk Edge or a Chair Back

Clamps onto a table edge, a desk lip or a chair backrest and gets the backpack off the floor. One part, no supports, no screws, no glue. Three sizes included — 20, 25 and 30 mm jaws — and a sprung tongue inside each takes up the slack below that, so between them they cover roughly 10 to 30 mm. A 7-minute fit-test coupon is included for every size so you can check the grip on your own table before printing the whole thing. The source file is included and fully parametric: change one number and you get a jaw for any thickness.
