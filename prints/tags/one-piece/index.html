Bathroom / Makeup organizers

Dragon Wing Palette Rack — eyeshadow palettes on edge, no supports

An eyeshadow-palette rack whose fins are the finger bones of a dragon wing. Palettes stand on edge like books, so you read the spines and pull one out one-handed instead of excavating a stack. Five 16.5 mm slots take anything up to an 18 mm palette, with 100 mm of clear depth between the front rail and the back spine and a 76 mm shortest fin. Every segment of the fin profile is upward-facing, so a 112 mm tall part prints with ZERO supports. 1 part, 118 x 128 x 112 mm, no supports, no hardware, no assembly.

Bathroom / Drains

Drain Hair Snake — Barbed Clog Remover

Flat flexible barbed drain stick that rakes hair clogs out of a shower, bath, or sink drain. Push the tip down the drain, twist, and pull — backward-pointing harpoon barbs grab the hair and drag it back out. One-piece print, no supports, no assembly. Finger-loop handle doubles as a hang hook and a stop so it cannot vanish down the drain.

Bathroom / Washing & personal care

Soap Dish — Wooden-Slat Cradle, Parametric Wood-Grain

Single-piece slatted soap dish that cradles oval and brick bars without rolling. Five saddle planks carry an oval bar; two flat fenders at each end keep larger blocks from migrating off. Drainage runs straight through the slat gaps onto the counter (or into a tray). Parametric wood-grain grooves on the slat sides — prints best in wood-fiber PLA for a real wooden look.

Toys & mechanisms / Whistles

Beawhistle — beaver whistle, loud twin-chamber, one piece, no supports

A beaver standing on its tail with a chewed stick in its teeth that is also a loud pealess whistle: blow into the stick, the sound leaves two slots between its shoulder blades. Two stopped-pipe chambers of unequal length pulse against each other — measured on the printed part at 2205 Hz with a ~70 Hz warble, the Fox-40 architecture. 99 x 26 x 50 mm, one piece, print-in-place, no supports, no hardware.
