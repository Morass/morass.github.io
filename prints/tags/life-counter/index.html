Custom d12 — a fair gaming die and a spindown counter, 18 mm

One 18 mm twelve-sided die in two numberings: a fair gaming d12 whose opposite faces sum to 13, and a spindown counter where every consecutive pair of numbers shares an edge so one roll of the die is one step. Prints flat on a pentagon with no supports. Single-colour for paint-fill, or two-colour on an AMS.

MTG — Mana Barrel Counter (3-Drum Hex Dial)

Three-digit (0-999) rotating barrel counter for Magic: the Gathering — hex prism with three independently-rotating drums, capped at each end by a hex cap engraved with the chosen mana symbol (W/U/B/R/G or colourless). Caps lock to a shared square axle so the drums rotate but the caps don't. Engraved digits + mana symbols print as 2-AMS Bambu Split-to-Parts. ~50 mm tall, no supports, PLA.

MTG Mana Counter Set — 6 colors, twin-dial

Snap-together MTG mana / life counters, one per colour (W U B R G C). Twin dials behind a window track 0-99 with a thumb-twist on the exposed dial rim. Two-colour AMS print, no fasteners.
