Bathroom / Makeup organizers

Apothecary Brush Drying Rack — hangs brushes bristles-down

A rack that dries makeup brushes the way every brush-care guide says to dry them: hanging HEAD DOWN over a drip tray. A brush stood bristles-up runs water into the ferrule, the glue softens and the brush sheds — this is the one makeup problem with a real failure mode behind it, and nothing prints a rack that fixes it. The grip is a WEDGE SLOT, not a hole: handles run about 6–12 mm and no single bore fits them, so each of the six slots tapers 13 → 5 mm over 22 mm and every brush catches at its own diameter, at about 9.4° per side, well inside PLA's friction angle. 172 mm of measured hang clears a 190 mm brush. The rail lifts straight off the two posts to load, and the tray is then a plain open dish you tip and rinse. 2 parts, 176 × 78 × 219 mm, 301 g, no supports, no hardware, no glue.

Board games / Dice

Potion-Vial Dice — Rolling d10 / d100 + Apothecary Display Stand (OpenSCAD)

Rolling-log d10 dice shaped like alchemist's potion vials — faceted glass body, tapered neck and cork. Roll one like a log and it always settles on a flat face; a units vial (1–10) plus a tens vial (00–90) roll percentile d100. Three-colour AMS (potion + numerals + cork) via Split-to-Parts, or single-colour with paint-fill grooves. Ships with a 6-bay arched apothecary display stand engraved with the classical-element sigils. Face labels are fully parametric. NOTE: the stand is a decorative display — vials rest loosely in open sockets, it is not a carry case.

Potion of Holding — Twist-Lock Flask Organizer (2 sizes)

A fantasy potion flask that twist-locks apart into a wide-mouth bin with a 4-way divider — four sorted compartments for screws, bolts, beads, pills, or tiny treasure. A friction cork plugs the neck. Two sizes from the same design: a full-size desk organizer and a mini that fits inside a 2.7-inch gacha capsule.
