Boxes & storage / Organizers / Baskets

Stackable Lattice Basket — 4 Sizes + Parametric SCAD

Cross-stackable lattice baskets with cone-peg corner posts — any basket stacks onto any other basket. Four ready-to-print sizes from 5×5×5 cm cube up to a 20×12×8 cm tray, plus an optional cone-foot leg accessory. One open short side per basket so you can reach contents while the stack stays put. Parametric SCAD included — override basket_l/basket_w/body_h to derive any size; corner-post + peg geometry is shared so derived sizes still cross-stack with the printed ones.

Desk & office / Electronics / Stands

Stackable Server Rack — Tool-Free Mini-PC / NUC / SBC Shelf

A modular, tool-free rack for mini-PCs, NUCs, SBCs and other small servers. Each level is a honeycomb-vented floor on four fat corner posts; levels self-stack on cone pegs (no screws, no hardware) and four wide feet lift the whole tower off the desk for airflow. Print one floor per device, four feet once, stack as tall as you like. Parametric SCAD — resize for any box.

Gothic Stone Throne — decorative sculpture

A miniature gothic stone throne: a spired skull-and-scroll backrest, sweeping wave/dragon-wing armrests, and a stepped rune plinth. ~143 × 146 × 211 mm — a chunky display sculpture for a desk, shelf, or D&D table. Prints as one solid watertight body.
