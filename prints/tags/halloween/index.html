Bathroom / Makeup organizers

Cauldron Makeup Organizer — a bowl with no corners, no supports

A three-legged cauldron that sorts makeup, and the bowl is the whole idea: it is a truncated sphere, so there is no corner anywhere inside it for powder to pack into. A 2.6 mm wall runs into a 3 mm floor through a 4 mm fillet rather than a crease, and one wipe reaches every part of the inside. Brushes stand in it bristles-up and fan out against the curve; a 4-way cross drops in to quarter the bowl for jars, sponges, compacts and hair ties, and lifts out to leave a plain brush cup. The three legs are a separate trivet ring the bowl drops into on a 45 degree cone, because legs cast under the bowl print as a horizontal overhang between them. 3 parts, 92 x 90 x 77 mm assembled, 117 g, no supports, no hardware, no glue — assembly is dropping one part into another.

Jewelry & accessories / Earrings

Raven Skull Earring — flat silhouette dangle, mirrored pair, no supports

A raven (Corvus corax) skull in lateral profile as a flat dangle earring: a big domed braincase pierced by an Ø8 orbit, the heavy decurved corvid culmen with an open nare, and a mandible fused only at the articulation so an open jaw slot runs the length of the beak — that slot is what makes it read as a skull rather than a bird head. One flat piece, ~25 x 41 x 1.8 mm, 0.93 g, printed face-up with zero overhangs and no supports. Ships mirrored left and right STLs so a worn pair both faces outward, and a fully parametric OpenSCAD source: size, thickness, hang angle, orbit and nare, bail hole.

Jewelry & accessories / Earrings

Spider Drop Earring — the spider actually SWINGS on its dragline

An orb web with a spider hanging below it on a dragline that is a real pivot, not a printed line. The dragline's top end is an eye captured on a post at the web hub and closed by a cap, so the spider is a free pendulum that swings side to side as you move. Print-in-place: one flat plate, two bodies, no supports, nothing to assemble. 20.0 x 40.1 x 4.9 mm, ~0.6 g. Print two for a pair.
