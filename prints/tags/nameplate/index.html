Classroom Desk Name Plate — slide-in letter tiles, one alphabet sheet spells the whole class

A school desk name plate whose name is not baked into the STL. One dovetail track, one tile per letter, threaded in from the open end. Print one alphabet sheet for the whole class and one plate per desk — re-spell it when the seating plan changes instead of re-slicing a custom model for every child. 41 tiles (A-Z plus Czech accented capitals), blanks that centre the name, an end stop that locks the row, and a pair of brackets that stand the plate up as a tent. Every letter also ships as its own file, plain and two-colour. No hardware, no glue, no supports.

Desk & office / Organizers / Paper Notes

Stacking Letter / A4 Paper Tray — modular, honeycomb floor, 20 swappable nameplates

A classic 4-post stacking in-out tray for A4 or Letter paper, sized to fit a 256 mm bed. Paper sits landscape and overhangs the low open front so you can grab a sheet one-handed. Stack as many tiers as you print — corner pegs drop into the tier below. Comes with a pack of 20 clip-on nameplates (IN · OUT · TODO · URGENT · BILLS · CHAOS …) plus a blank one to write on. No screws, no glue, no supports.

Lace Dubrae — Custom Sneaker Lace Name Plates (parametric, 48 names + your own)

Sneaker lace dubraes — the little name plate that rides on your shoelaces. The lace runs hidden through one big channel that exits both ends, so the top stays a clean, curved name plate (it bows lengthwise like the real metal ones). One shared BODY hosts every name: print the body once, then drop any text plate on top in a contrast filament. Ships the body plus 48 ready-made names AND the full parametric .scad so you can type any word and re-render. Prints dead flat, no supports.
