Bathroom / Makeup organizers

Apothecary Brush Drying Rack — hangs brushes bristles-down

A rack that dries makeup brushes the way every brush-care guide says to dry them: hanging HEAD DOWN over a drip tray. A brush stood bristles-up runs water into the ferrule, the glue softens and the brush sheds — this is the one makeup problem with a real failure mode behind it, and nothing prints a rack that fixes it. The grip is a WEDGE SLOT, not a hole: handles run about 6–12 mm and no single bore fits them, so each of the six slots tapers 13 → 5 mm over 22 mm and every brush catches at its own diameter, at about 9.4° per side, well inside PLA's friction angle. 172 mm of measured hang clears a 190 mm brush. The rail lifts straight off the two posts to load, and the tray is then a plain open dish you tip and rinse. 2 parts, 176 × 78 × 219 mm, 301 g, no supports, no hardware, no glue.

Bathroom / Makeup organizers

Dragon Wing Palette Rack — eyeshadow palettes on edge, no supports

An eyeshadow-palette rack whose fins are the finger bones of a dragon wing. Palettes stand on edge like books, so you read the spines and pull one out one-handed instead of excavating a stack. Five 16.5 mm slots take anything up to an 18 mm palette, with 100 mm of clear depth between the front rail and the back spine and a 76 mm shortest fin. Every segment of the fin profile is upward-facing, so a 112 mm tall part prints with ZERO supports. 1 part, 118 x 128 x 112 mm, no supports, no hardware, no assembly.

Jewelry & accessories / Earrings

Raven Skull Earring — flat silhouette dangle, mirrored pair, no supports

A raven (Corvus corax) skull in lateral profile as a flat dangle earring: a big domed braincase pierced by an Ø8 orbit, the heavy decurved corvid culmen with an open nare, and a mandible fused only at the articulation so an open jaw slot runs the length of the beak — that slot is what makes it read as a skull rather than a bird head. One flat piece, ~25 x 41 x 1.8 mm, 0.93 g, printed face-up with zero overhangs and no supports. Ships mirrored left and right STLs so a worn pair both faces outward, and a fully parametric OpenSCAD source: size, thickness, hang angle, orbit and nare, bail hole.
