MTG — Mana Barrel Counter (3-Drum Hex Dial)

Three-digit (0-999) rotating barrel counter for Magic: the Gathering — hex prism with three independently-rotating drums, capped at each end by a hex cap engraved with the chosen mana symbol (W/U/B/R/G or colourless). Caps lock to a shared square axle so the drums rotate but the caps don't. Engraved digits + mana symbols print as 2-AMS Bambu Split-to-Parts. ~50 mm tall, no supports, PLA.

MTG Deck Box with Mana Symbol Lid

Simple cap-lid deck box for sleeved Commander-sized MTG decks. Lid has an embossed mana symbol (WUBRG); optional second-color inlay STL for two-colour AMS printing.

Jewelry & accessories / Sewing buttons

MTG Mana Buttons — all six mana symbols as real sewing buttons, 15 / 20 / 25 mm, hidden shank or 2-hole

Wearable sewing buttons carrying all six Magic mana symbols — white sun, blue drop, black skull, red fireball, green tree and the colorless lozenge. 30 STLs: 6 symbols x 5 size/style combos. The 'tunnel' style hides a thread shank in the underside so the face stays whole and nothing is bored through the emblem; the 'holed' style is a classic 2-hole shirt button with both bores in the plain ring. Ø15 / 20 / 25 mm (24L / 32L / 40L). Every button ships a named-solid 2-colour companion for AMS. Prints flat, face up, no supports, no brim, no hardware. Fully parametric OpenSCAD — any diameter, and the glyph strokes are held at or above one nozzle width automatically.

MTG Powerstone Token — Flip-to-Tap Crystal

Chunky 60 × 30 × 16 mm faceted crystal token for Magic: the Gathering's Powerstone artifacts. It rests flat on either octagonal face: plain side up is untapped, tap-arrow side up is tapped. Flip it instead of rotating it. Prints as one part with no supports.
