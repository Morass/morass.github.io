Domino Set — Double-Six (28 tiles), 2-Colour Engraved (OpenSCAD)

Full double-six domino set: 28 tiles, 36 × 18 × 5.5 mm each, Ø2.4 mm engraved pips + centre line. Two-colour print via Bambu AMS — combined named-solid STL (Split-to-Parts) ships alongside separate body + pips STLs for non-Bambu printers. Parametric SCAD included so you can re-tune tile size, pip diameter, or face content (single-tile mode `left`/`right` lets you reprint any face).

Board games / Dice

Custom Stroke Dice — a d20 + d6 you read by counting strokes

A d20 and a d6 numbered in a custom stroke script instead of digits: strokes cut across a centre line, and where they sit plus how many there are gives the value. Nothing to memorise — count the strokes, read their angle. Prints single-colour (crisp recesses, ready for paint-fill) or two-colour on an AMS.

Board games / Dice

Tengwar d6 — a 20 mm die numbered in elvish duodecimal numerals

A 20 mm six-sided die whose pips are replaced by authentic Tengwar duodecimal numerals. Opposite faces sum to 7 and the 1-2-3 corner is the standard right-handed arrangement, so it rolls like any ordinary d6 — it just reads elvish. Print it single colour and paint-fill the recesses, or drop in the coordinate-locked numeral plugs for a two-colour AMS print. Printed and verified.

Monarch Crown Token (MTG) — 3-Colour Heraldic Crown, OpenSCAD

Heraldic crown token for the Monarch mechanic in Magic: The Gathering. Slow-cylinder bell band + 7 tall triangular spikes alternating with 7 short crenel spikes, Ø5 mm orb finials on each tall tip. "MONARCH" wraps around the band as inset 2-colour text. 3-colour print via Bambu AMS — combined named-solid STL (Split-to-Parts) ships alongside per-filament STLs.

Infinity Cube — Animal Fidget (folding magic cube, 48 engraved animals, two-colour)

A folding infinity cube (magic cube) fidget toy: eight cubelets linked so you can fold it end over end forever. Every one of the 48 faces carries a different animal silhouette. Prints fully assembled — the links are captured by the cube geometry, not glued — and the animals come as a separate inlay STL so you can print them in a contrast colour. Confirmed printed and everting on a real machine.
