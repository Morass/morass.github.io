Bathroom / Makeup organizers

Wizard Tower Makeup Organizer — 4 lift-apart tiers

A wizard's tower that sorts makeup. Brushes hang bristles-up from five wedge bores through the roof cone, so each one catches at its own handle diameter and a 120 mm and a 190 mm brush stand at the same height. The balcony deck takes a mascara, two lipsticks and three pencils; the plinth is a deep open bin for polish and jars. Every one of the eleven openings is a THROUGH hole and the bin floor is coved into the wall, so powder falls through instead of packing into a blind corner: the tower lifts apart into four pieces you tip and rinse under a tap. No supports, no glue, no hardware — every joint is a triangular tongue that drops into a V-groove.

Board games / Accessories / Tokens & stands

Meeple Set — Humans & Animals

A big set of classic wooden-style meeples: six human figures (plain worker, warrior, king, wizard, archer, axeman) in several sizes, plus eight animal pawns (cat, rabbit, dog, mouse, sheep, goat, duck, horse). Chunky flat tokens that print flat with zero supports — drop-in replacements and extras for almost any board game.

Board games / Find your game / Dungeons & Dragons

Spell-Slot Abacus — print-in-place D&D spell-slot tracker

A print-in-place abacus for tracking D&D spell slots: 9 rails (spell levels 1–9) of captive sliding crystal beads (4/3/3/3/3/2/2/1/1 = full-caster max). Slide a bead to burn a slot, sweep back on a long rest. A tilt-lock detent at mid-span keeps the count from scrambling when you pick the board up. ~107 × 130 × 8 mm, zero supports.

Decorations / Magnets

Deathly Hallows Fridge Magnet

The three Deathly Hallows as a slim fridge magnet — Cloak triangle, Resurrection-Stone circle, Elder-Wand line. Three Ø5×3 mm magnets are sealed invisibly inside the rounded corners during a single print pause: no glue, no exposed metal, either face sticks to the fridge.

Decorations / Magnets

Wizard Figurine — robed mage with a magnet hand

A robed wizard figurine with a pointed wide-brim hat, long beard and one hand raised in greeting. The raised hand has a recessed slot for a small disc magnet, so he can hold a metal wand, pin or trinket. It is NOT a fridge magnet — the figure will not stick to a fridge, and the little magnet is weak, so think of it as a charming desk or shelf wizard with a handy magnetic hand. Ships as an STL plus a ready-to-slice Bambu .3mf.

Desk & office / Organizers / Pen Holders

Wizard Tower Pen Holder — fantasy castle desk organizer

A squat stone wizard's tower that holds your pens and pencils. Crenellated battlement crown, spiral arrow-slit windows and a flared buttressed base; the bored centre well swallows a fistful of pens so they jut out the top like spires. Prints upright, no supports, fully parametric.
