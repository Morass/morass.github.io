Articulated Worm Dangle Earring — Print-in-Place Flexi Worm

A tiny ~38 mm print-in-place worm that actually wiggles and sways when worn. Five segments linked by captive teardrop ball-socket joints — printed flat, belly-down, NO supports, then flexed free. Friendly face with eyes + an engraved smile, and a flat ear loop in the bed plane so it hangs in classic worm side profile. Print twice for a pair.

Chainmail Coaster — 8 cm flexible print-in-place grid

80×80 mm drink coaster built from 11×11 print-in-place cube hubs, each linked to its neighbours by tilted ring pairs. Prints in one go, comes off the bed as a flexible chainmail fabric that drapes under a glass.

Toys & mechanisms / Fidgets

Flexi Snake — print-in-place articulated fidget that coils

A print-in-place snake that prints in one piece, no supports, and coils into a full spiral in your hand. 16 necked vertebrae on captive ball-and-stalk joints, each folding 50-70 degrees, plus a carved head with eyes and scale rings. Fully parametric: dial the number of segments and their length right in the .scad.

Tank Shooter — chunky display tank, print-in-place tracks

A chunky stylised T-34-ish display tank: articulated print-in-place link tracks that wrap the road wheels, a rotating turret, and a cosmetic gun with a friction-seated dummy shell. No glue, no supports, no hardware, AMS multi-colour. A DISPLAY model — the tracks don't drive and the gun doesn't fire.
