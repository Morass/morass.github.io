Toys & mechanisms / Whistles

Beawhistle — beaver whistle, loud twin-chamber, one piece, no supports

A beaver standing on its tail with a chewed stick in its teeth that is also a loud pealess whistle: blow into the stick, the sound leaves two slots between its shoulder blades. Two stopped-pipe chambers of unequal length pulse against each other — measured on the printed part at 2205 Hz with a ~70 Hz warble, the Fox-40 architecture. 99 x 26 x 50 mm, one piece, print-in-place, no supports, no hardware.

Toys & mechanisms / Fidgets

Flexi Snake — print-in-place articulated fidget that coils

A print-in-place snake that prints in one piece, no supports, and coils into a full spiral in your hand. 16 necked vertebrae on captive ball-and-stalk joints, each folding 50-70 degrees, plus a carved head with eyes and scale rings. Fully parametric: dial the number of segments and their length right in the .scad.

Toys & mechanisms / Mechanical

Wand Through the Wall — a Straight Wand Passes Through a Curved Slit

A dead-straight wand on a little turntable swings clean through two CURVED slits in a wall, twice per turn, without bending. The wand is skew to the axis, so it sweeps a hyperboloid; the wall cuts that in a hyperbola, and that is the slit. Four flat supportless parts, formula engraved on the base.

Toys & mechanisms / Fidgets

Infinity Cube — Animal Fidget (folding magic cube, 48 engraved animals, two-colour)

A folding infinity cube (magic cube) fidget toy: eight cubelets linked so you can fold it end over end forever. Every one of the 48 faces carries a different animal silhouette. Prints fully assembled — the links are captured by the cube geometry, not glued — and the animals come as a separate inlay STL so you can print them in a contrast colour. Confirmed printed and everting on a real machine.

Beach Ring-Toss Lawn Game — Scoring Target Board + Tossing Rings (sand-resting, no stakes)
