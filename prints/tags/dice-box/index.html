Mimic Vault — dice drawer in a monster that eats the loot

A ruined stone monument that's secretly a mimic: the front drawer is its maw and slides out to reveal your 7-die set, relief teeth grin under a translucent crystal bait pile on top. Prints upright, no supports. Includes a matching two-colour D6 with bone-femur Roman numerals.

Library of Alexandria box — a magnetic box under the great dome

The Library of Alexandria as a box: a white-marble hall behind an arcade of pointed arches, a full-width stair, four turrets under gilded onion domes and a great ribbed dome on a chevroned drum. Everything above the cornice is the lid, and eight Ø5 × 3 mm magnets buried mid-print hold it shut. 118 × 96 × 113 mm outside, 90 × 60 × 45 mm inside. Six STLs, two prints, no supports, no glue.

Castle keep box — a fortified keep that opens at the wall-walk

A 112 mm square castle keep whose lid is the wall-walk: lift the battlements, four corner drums and their tiled conical roofs straight off and the keep is a 70 x 70 x 64 mm box. Two prints, no supports, no hardware, nothing to glue. The lid rests rather than latches, so it is a table box, not a travelling one. Multi-colour for the AMS, and it still reads as a castle in one filament.

Grimoire Box — a hinged spellbook with the magnets sealed inside

A leather-bound tome that opens on a real barrel hinge. Two sizes, any word you like on the cover, and four magnets that end up fully enclosed in the plastic - nothing visible in any face. Three printed parts, no supports, no screws, no glue.

Rhombic Dodecahedron Box — one part printed twice, magnets sealed inside

A 64 mm rhombic dodecahedron that opens at its equator. ONE STL, printed twice — the halves are congruent, not mirrored. Eight Ø4×2 magnets are enclosed mid-print: no glue, nothing visible, nothing to fall out. Stacks like cannonballs.
