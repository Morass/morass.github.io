MTG — Amass Army Dial (0–15, goblin knob)

A two-part rotating counter for a single growing Army token. Only ONE number is ever visible, through a window in the fixed cap, so it reads like a tally slate rather than a clock face. Thumb on the goblin holds the cap, fingers turn the scalloped rim, every number lands with a detent click. Two printed parts, no glue, no hardware, no supports.

MTG Mana Counter Set — 6 colors, twin-dial

Snap-together MTG mana / life counters, one per colour (W U B R G C). Twin dials behind a window track 0-99 with a thumb-twist on the exposed dial rim. Two-colour AMS print, no fasteners.

MTG — Map Token Counter (1-10 Compass Dial)

Physical MTG Map artifact token that doubles as a 1-10 counter, styled as a pocket compass. 56 mm two-colour face with numerals, tick ring and engraved compass rose. The two-tone needle presses over the centre post ONCE and clicks captive — then spins freely forever. Bambu AMS-friendly (split-to-parts named solids).

MTG Undercity Dungeon — Hex pyramid prop

Physical play prop for the MTG Undercity dungeon (Commander Legends: Baldur's Gate). 9 hex rooms with engraved effects, doors on the canonical 12 connections, peg-mounted player pins. Two-colour AMS-friendly.
