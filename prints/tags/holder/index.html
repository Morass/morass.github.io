MagDock — Magnetic Desk & Shop Organizer Family

A family of small organizers that embed neodymium discs in their backs, so they snap onto ANY ferrous surface — printer frame, fridge, toolbox, filing cabinet, radiator. Hook, tray, tall caddy, pliers/scissors slots, and a bare puck. The magnets are sealed inside DURING printing (mid-print pause), so no caps and no glue. Every part prints flat on its back with NO supports.
