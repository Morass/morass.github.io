Board games / Find your game / Dungeons & Dragons

D&D Death Save Tracker — slide up for a heart, down for a skull

A flat, silent death-saving-throw tracker for D&D 5e. Three captive sliders: push one UP and a red heart fills the top window (success), push it DOWN and a white skull fills the bottom one (failure), leave it centred and both stay blank. Three hearts you're stable, three skulls you're dead — the whole table can read your state from across it. Five printed parts, no glue, no screws, no dice-sized bits to lose; everything prints flat and supportless. Parametric OpenSCAD source included.

Board games / Dice

Pasa — Indian long dice, the d4 you can actually roll

A long die: a square rod marked on its four long sides and capped at both ends so it can never stand up. You roll it like a pencil and read the number on top. Ships the traditional Chaupar/Pachisi stave (faces 1-2-5-6) and a plain 1-2-3-4 version, each in one colour and as a two-colour Split-to-Parts file. 62 x 10 x 10 mm, flat on the bed, no supports. The opposite-face pairing is chosen to MINIMISE bias, not to sum to seven — 4.1x less centre-of-mass offset, and the two-colour build cancels it entirely.
