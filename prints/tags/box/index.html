Bathroom / Makeup organizers

Tenshu Makeup Organizer — Japanese castle keep, 3 lift-off storeys

A Japanese castle keep (tenshu) that sorts makeup. Every roof is the lid of the storey under it: the base holds four compacts on edge in a lift-out comb plus a polish bay, the second storey has three coved lanes for a mascara, lipsticks and pencils lying down, the third a tweezer lane and two cells for lashes, pins and rings. Every pocket is a hull of four spheres, so the floor meets the wall on a 4 mm cove and nothing is a square corner or a blind hole: lift, lift, lift, tip, rinse. Five parts, no supports, no glue, no hardware — each lid seats on a 2.4 mm ring so the stack does not rock, and magnets are an option, not a need. Ships as one-colour STLs and, for a multi-filament printer, as one three-colour STL per part that Split to Parts divides into exactly three: cream plaster, dark timber, slate stone-and-tile. A five-colour split-to-parts STL per part ships too, if you have the slots for a separate plinth grey and gold shachihoko.

Domino Box — Sleeve + Tray for the Double-Six 28-Tile Set (OpenSCAD)

Two-piece storage for a full 28-tile double-six domino set. Outer sleeve (closed on 5 faces, open on one short end) + inner tray with a Ø28 mm semicircular thumb-notch on its front wall. Tiles lie flat in a 4×2 grid of 4-tile stacks (32-slot capacity). Sleeve prints with the open mouth UP — fully supportless. Engraved decorative pip pattern on every long face. Parametric SCAD lets you re-tune tile dimensions, slop, layout (piles_x × piles_y), pip pattern, or wall thicknesses.

Moku Iris Box — Working Camera-Aperture Catch-All (Base Rail module)

A round fluted catch-all box for the Moku modular desk system whose lid is a WORKING 6-blade iris diaphragm — twist the knurled collar and the blades spiral open like a camera aperture onto the cavity (Ø48 mm) for clips, SD cards and coins. Same-plane pinwheel iris: 6 identical rigid blades on pivot posts, drive pins riding radial collar slots, snap-on collar, 1-minute no-order assembly, zero supports. Clips onto the host Base Rail by the reverse-measured foot interface. A remix of the Moku Base Rail by @SabreDesign / @Collecticraft.
