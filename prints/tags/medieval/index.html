Bathroom / Makeup organizers

Great Helm Makeup Organizer — knight's helm, no supports

A 13th-century flat-topped great helm that is a makeup organizer on the inside. The crown is open and a lift-out liner drops onto the rim carrying the whole bore field, so brushes stand out of the helm like a crest: six brush bores go straight THROUGH so the handle hangs in the cavity and a 120 mm and a 220 mm brush stand at the same height, three tube bores (Ø27 mascara, two Ø23 lipstick) neck down instead of bottoming out, and an 88 × 24 mm palette slot rests a compact on an open 8 mm ledge. Not one bore is blind, so powder falls through into a smooth drum you tip out and rinse. 2 parts, 134 × 125 × 150 mm, 307 g, no supports, no hardware, no glue.

Board games / Find your game / Magic: The Gathering / Tokens

MTG — Pact Reminder Token (YOU WILL PAY Scroll)

Medieval-scroll reminder token for Magic: the Gathering. Drop in front of an opponent who owes you a Pact upkeep (Pact of Negation, Slaughter Pact, Intervention Pact, Pact of the Titans, Summoner's Pact) or anyone trying to skip a delayed cost. 55×80 mm slim slab with raised parchment-roll ridges top + bottom and 'YOU WILL PAY' in raised medieval lettering. Two-colour AMS print: parchment body + ink letters.

Boxes & storage / Keepsake boxes / Fantasy

Castle keep box — a fortified keep that opens at the wall-walk

A 112 mm square castle keep whose lid is the wall-walk: lift the battlements, four corner drums and their tiled conical roofs straight off and the keep is a 70 x 70 x 64 mm box. Two prints, no supports, no hardware, nothing to glue. The lid rests rather than latches, so it is a table box, not a travelling one. Multi-colour for the AMS, and it still reads as a castle in one filament.
