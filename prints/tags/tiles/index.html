Breach Dungeon Tiles — modular D&D base squares + drop-in terrain

A modular dungeon-building kit of snap-together base square tiles plus everything that drops into them: floor-texture inserts (stone, cracked ground, cobbles, rubble, fissures), wall pieces, and scatter props (chests, barrels, statues, trees, crystals). Every insert, wall and prop sits on the same 27.9 mm square base. Prints support-free. Pairs with any 28 mm minis.

The Older Current — a board game for PlayGrid

A complete 2-4 player strategy game on the PlayGrid grid — board, 100 tiles, 12 figures and a box, all included. You never move your own people; you re-aim the current that carries them.

The Everfruit Vale — a competitive and cooperative board game for PlayGrid

A tile-laying game with two modes in one box. Draft from four face-up tiles, place two a turn, and plant Grovekeepers in the root networks you want to score. Competitive for 2-4 or cooperative for 1-4, 35-50 minutes, on an existing PlayGrid 10x10 board.
