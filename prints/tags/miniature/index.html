Adventurer Dial Base — a mini base that counts your damage 0–9

A Ø36 mm miniature base with a rotating 0–9 counter ring built into it. It prints in place: the disc and the ring come off the plate already interlocked, no assembly, no hardware. A hooded wanderer figure is included — drop it in, or print the version with it welded on.

Board games / Accessories / Tokens & stands

Meeple & Standee Stand Base — Friction Slot Holder for Thin Tokens (Parametric, OpenSCAD)

A weighted clip base that holds any THIN game token upright — cardboard punchboard standees, acrylic or printed silhouettes, even a card edge. The token drops into a friction slot with a flared insertion mouth; the wide footprint resists tipping in the easy-to-tip direction (across the slot). Optional vertical grip ribs pinch flimsy card so it can't flop. One slider — slot_w — tunes the slot to YOUR token thickness (~1.6 acrylic / 2.2 standard cardboard / ~7 a printed meeple slab). Prints flat, slot UP, zero supports. Single base or a batch plate.

Board games / Find your game / Terraforming Mars

Terraforming Mars Track Figures — Biodome, Reactor, Rover

Three sculpted markers for the Terraforming Mars tracks: a greenhouse biodome for oxygen, a fluted cooling tower for temperature and a six-wheel rover for generation. Each is one solid print on a Ø12 mm base, upright with no supports, no assembly and no hardware — about 2.2 g of PLA for the whole set. Print one filament per figure; they replace the game's ordinary markers on its existing printed tracks.

Mini Anvil Earring — 3D Blacksmith Anvil Charm

A tiny London-pattern anvil earring, modelled fully in the round — tapered horn, overhanging working face, waisted body and spread base — with a pierced heel eye for a jump ring. Prints supportless on its side. Parametric OpenSCAD source included.

Jewelry & accessories / Earrings

Tiny Book Earring — a wearable book that really opens, print-in-place hinge, no supports

A book small enough to wear that actually opens. One print-in-place piece: two covers on a seven-knuckle piano hinge with a captive pin, a hollow page cavity inside, and ONCE UPON engraved on the inner cover so the message only shows when you open it. 22 x 17.6 x 4.2 mm closed, 1.55 g, about 25 minutes, no supports and no hardware beyond an ear wire. Comes off the plate open flat; fold it once and it clicks shut on a printed detent bead, and a thumbnail in the fore-edge scallop pops it open again. Fully parametric OpenSCAD source — size, cover thickness, hinge clearance, detent grip and the engraved message are all variables.
