Bathroom / Makeup organizers

Wizard Tower Makeup Organizer — 4 lift-apart tiers

A wizard's tower that sorts makeup. Brushes hang bristles-up from five wedge bores through the roof cone, so each one catches at its own handle diameter and a 120 mm and a 190 mm brush stand at the same height. The balcony deck takes a mascara, two lipsticks and three pencils; the plinth is a deep open bin for polish and jars. Every one of the eleven openings is a THROUGH hole and the bin floor is coved into the wall, so powder falls through instead of packing into a blind corner: the tower lifts apart into four pieces you tip and rinse under a tap. No supports, no glue, no hardware — every joint is a triangular tongue that drops into a V-groove.

Boxes & storage / Keepsake boxes / Fantasy

Castle keep box — a fortified keep that opens at the wall-walk

A 112 mm square castle keep whose lid is the wall-walk: lift the battlements, four corner drums and their tiled conical roofs straight off and the keep is a 70 x 70 x 64 mm box. Two prints, no supports, no hardware, nothing to glue. The lid rests rather than latches, so it is a table box, not a travelling one. Multi-colour for the AMS, and it still reads as a castle in one filament.

Wizard Tower Pen Holder — fantasy castle desk organizer

A squat stone wizard's tower that holds your pens and pencils. Crenellated battlement crown, spiral arrow-slit windows and a flared buttressed base; the bored centre well swallows a fistful of pens so they jut out the top like spires. Prints upright, no supports, fully parametric.
