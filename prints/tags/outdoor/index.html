Outdoors / Beach Games

Beach Ring-Toss Lawn Game — Scoring Target Board + Tossing Rings (sand-resting, no stakes)

A beach/garden ring-toss set: a flat sand-resting target board studded with 9 scoring pins (centre = 3 pts, 3 inner = 2 pts, 5 outer = 1 pt) plus chunky two-team tossing rings. Toss a ring underhand so it drops over a pin to score. Print two boards and set them a few metres apart. Fully parametric OpenSCAD — every diameter, height and ring size is a variable. Prints flat with no supports.

Outdoors / Sand Play

Beach Sandcastle Kit — 8-Piece Mould & Tool Set (castle buckets + sieve, rake, shovel, tamper)

A complete kids' beach sandcastle kit: 3 stacking castle bucket-moulds (keep, tower, great hall) plus 5 sand tools — a perforated sieve-scoop, a rake, a shovel, a tamper and a detailing tool. Pack the wet sand into a mould, lift, and decorate with the tools. All parametric OpenSCAD, prints flat with no supports.

Outdoors / Beach Games

Beach Tic-Tac-Toe in a Scallop-Shell Box — Sea-Creature Peg Game (starfish vs shells)

A pocket 3x3 peg tic-tac-toe for the beach that lives inside an openable scallop-shell clamshell box. The lid is the play board (pieces plug into its 9 holes, friction fit, so wind can't blow them off); the base is a plain storage cup, so the loose pieces can't spill. 5 starfish vs 5 scallop shells (equal counts, so whoever starts both players have a full set). Fully parametric OpenSCAD; every part prints flat with no supports.

Toys & mechanisms / Mechanical

Squeeze Breeze — Trigger-Spun Fan Toy (no batteries, mechanical novelty)

A no-batteries mechanical novelty: squeeze the finger trigger and a multi-blade propeller on a flywheel spins up through a one-way ratchet, then coasts between squeezes. A satisfying hand-powered fidget that LOOKS like a hand fan. NOTE: it's a toy — the spinning blades move only a faint wisp of air, not a real cooling breeze. Multi-part, every part prints flat and supportless. Fully parametric OpenSCAD.
