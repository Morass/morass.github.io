Elven Motif Keepsake Box — vesica plan, interlaced vine walls, two-colour leaf inlay

A pointed-oval trinket box where every ornament is the same shape at three scales: the box plan, the leaves woven into the wall braid, and the veined leaf inlaid in the lid. The wall vine is a genuine over-and-under interlace, not two sine waves. Two printed parts, no supports, no hardware, no glue — and the two-colour lid costs about 1 g of purge because all the ink sits in three layers.
