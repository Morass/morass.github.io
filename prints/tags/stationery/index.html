Hedgehog Pen & Pencil Holder — your pens become the quills

A bald little hedgehog whose quills are YOUR pens and pencils. The smooth dome body has an angled fan of 11 blind bores drilled into its back; plug pens and pencils in and they radiate outward exactly like real quills. Dimple eyes and a snout finish the face. Prints belly-flat on the bed with zero supports — the dome is the top half of an ellipsoid so every wall leans inward going up. Single part, single colour, just print and load it up.
