Fly Agaric Mushroom Earring — 2-Color Charm

Parametric Amanita muscaria (fly agaric) earring charm — a red domed cap with scattered white warts on a white volva stem with a drooping veil ring. Two-color for AMS: the white spots are cut as complementary dimples in the red cap, so the colors tile perfectly with no overlap and no holes through the body. Prints standing, cap up, support-free. Ships a combined STL with 2 named solids for Split-to-Parts. Print twice for a pair.

Toadstool 🍄 — twist-open bonbon capsule

A tiny Amanita-muscaria toadstool that twists open to hide a wrapped bonbon or candy inside. The red spotted cap is a screw-off roof; underneath is a clean toadstool stalk — Amanita skirt ring and a soft volva bulb at the base — with a hollow chamber. Two-colour AMS cap. Closed capsule is Ø46 × 52 mm (min sphere Ø58 mm) — fits inside a 2.7-inch (68.6 mm) capsule. No supports, parametric OpenSCAD source included.
