Board games / Dice

Crooked Dice — a fair die, a gaff die and a loaded die that look identical

Three 16 mm d6 from one source: an honest die, a duplicate-face gaff showing only 5 and 6, and a die with a sealed void under the 6. Same body, same size, same weight to within a tenth of a gram. Ships the float-test cup that catches the loaded one, and states how crooked each die is as a measured number instead of an adjective — centre-of-mass offset and tipping-barrier ratio for all six faces. A magic and party prop, sold with its own counter-measure.

Jewelry & accessories / Sewing buttons

MTG Mana Buttons — all six mana symbols as real sewing buttons, 15 / 20 / 25 mm, hidden shank or 2-hole

Wearable sewing buttons carrying all six Magic mana symbols — white sun, blue drop, black skull, red fireball, green tree and the colorless lozenge. 30 STLs: 6 symbols x 5 size/style combos. The 'tunnel' style hides a thread shank in the underside so the face stays whole and nothing is bored through the emblem; the 'holed' style is a classic 2-hole shirt button with both bores in the plain ring. Ø15 / 20 / 25 mm (24L / 32L / 40L). Every button ships a named-solid 2-colour companion for AMS. Prints flat, face up, no supports, no brim, no hardware. Fully parametric OpenSCAD — any diameter, and the glyph strokes are held at or above one nozzle width automatically.

Marit Lage 20/20 Token — MTG flat-card display plinth with eldritch sculpt + multicolour text

A display plinth for the Marit Lage token from Magic: The Gathering (Dark Depths) — laid out like a Magic card lying flat (≈90 × 60 mm, low 4 mm base). A colossal Lovecraftian tentacle sculpt sits as the 'art', RAISED multicolour text reads MARIT LAGE on top, INDESTRUCTIBLE / FLYING in front of the art, and a big 20/20 power/toughness on the front vertical wall. Prints flat, plinth-on-bed, creature up — supportless. Toys & mechanisms / Mechanical

Wand Through the Wall — a Straight Wand Passes Through a Curved Slit

A dead-straight wand on a little turntable swings clean through two CURVED slits in a wall, twice per turn, without bending. The wand is skew to the axis, so it sweeps a hyperboloid; the wall cuts that in a hyperbola, and that is the slit. Four flat supportless parts, formula engraved on the base.
