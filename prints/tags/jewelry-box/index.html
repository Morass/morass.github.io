Elven Motif Keepsake Box — vesica plan, interlaced vine walls, two-colour leaf inlay

A pointed-oval trinket box where every ornament is the same shape at three scales: the box plan, the leaves woven into the wall braid, and the veined leaf inlaid in the lid. The wall vine is a genuine over-and-under interlace, not two sine waves. Two printed parts, no supports, no hardware, no glue — and the two-colour lid costs about 1 g of purge because all the ink sits in three layers.

Pirate treasure chest — print-in-place hinged trinket box, two colour

A 100 mm barrel-top pirate chest that comes off the bed as one working unit. The piano hinge prints in place, so there is no assembly, no pin to fit and no glue. Two-colour for the AMS: wood planks plus iron straps, banding, corner brackets, lock plate and hasp.

Rhombic Dodecahedron Box — one part printed twice, magnets sealed inside

A 64 mm rhombic dodecahedron that opens at its equator. ONE STL, printed twice — the halves are congruent, not mirrored. Eight Ø4×2 magnets are enclosed mid-print: no glue, nothing visible, nothing to fall out. Stacks like cannonballs.
