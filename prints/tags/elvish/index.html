Board games / Dice

Tengwar d6 — a 20 mm die numbered in elvish duodecimal numerals

A 20 mm six-sided die whose pips are replaced by authentic Tengwar duodecimal numerals. Opposite faces sum to 7 and the 1-2-3 corner is the standard right-handed arrangement, so it rolls like any ordinary d6 — it just reads elvish. Print it single colour and paint-fill the recesses, or drop in the coordinate-locked numeral plugs for a two-colour AMS print. Printed and verified.

Boxes & storage / Keepsake boxes / Fantasy

Elven Motif Keepsake Box — vesica plan, interlaced vine walls, two-colour leaf inlay

A pointed-oval trinket box where every ornament is the same shape at three scales: the box plan, the leaves woven into the wall braid, and the veined leaf inlaid in the lid. The wall vine is a genuine over-and-under interlace, not two sine waves. Two printed parts, no supports, no hardware, no glue — and the two-colour lid costs about 1 g of purge because all the ink sits in three layers.

Decorations / Clocks

Tengwar Wall Clock — Elvish Duodecimal Numerals

A Ø200 mm round wall clock with twelve authentic Tengwar duodecimal hour numerals and elvish leaf-blade hands. A real clock, not a flat disc: an integrated standoff back wall hides the quartz movement and a rear deep-channel keyhole hangs it on a single ordinary wall screw. Numerals + ticks can be paint-filled or printed as second/third AMS colours. Prints face-down, no supports.
