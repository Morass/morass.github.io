Q-tip Cup — Fluted Cylinder with Snap-Fit Lid

Ø69 × 60 mm fluted cup that holds ~250 standard cotton swabs, with a faceted gem-top lid that snaps on with an audible click. Doric-style vertical flutes (24 concave grooves) on the cup, octagonal gem dome on the lid. Two STLs, prints flat, no supports.

Around the home / Bowls

Twisted Vortex Bowl — one part, no supports

A catch-all bowl whose flutes spiral out of a small foot into a wide rim. The flutes climb at the same angle everywhere, so nothing on the outside of the bowl overhangs past 36 degrees and it prints with no supports and no brim. 46 mm foot, 78 mm rim, 45 mm tall, holds about 115 ml, around 30 g. Parametric OpenSCAD source included — twist, flare, flute pitch and depth are all named variables at the top.

Desk & office / Organizers / Paper Notes

Stacking Letter / A4 Paper Tray — modular, honeycomb floor, 20 swappable nameplates

A classic 4-post stacking in-out tray for A4 or Letter paper, sized to fit a 256 mm bed. Paper sits landscape and overhangs the low open front so you can grab a sheet one-handed. Stack as many tiers as you print — corner pegs drop into the tier below. Comes with a pack of 20 clip-on nameplates (IN · OUT · TODO · URGENT · BILLS · CHAOS …) plus a blank one to write on. No screws, no glue, no supports.

Desk & office / Organizers / Modular

Moku Iris Box — Working Camera-Aperture Catch-All (Base Rail module)

A round fluted catch-all box for the Moku modular desk system whose lid is a WORKING 6-blade iris diaphragm — twist the knurled collar and the blades spiral open like a camera aperture onto the cavity (Ø48 mm) for clips, SD cards and coins. Same-plane pinwheel iris: 6 identical rigid blades on pivot posts, drive pins riding radial collar slots, snap-on collar, 1-minute no-order assembly, zero supports. Clips onto the host Base Rail by the reverse-measured foot interface. A remix of the Moku Base Rail by @SabreDesign / @Collecticraft.

Desk & office / Organizers / Paper Notes

Moku Memo Stand — Sloped Sticky-Note Writing Tray (Base Rail module, 3 sizes)

A sloped sticky-note writing tray that clips onto the Moku modular desk-system Base Rail. The pad sits on a 21° incline so the top sheet faces you — read it and write on it in place. Low front rim plus a centre finger-scoop to peel the top sheet; tall back and side rims keep the pad square. Three pad sizes (60 / 80 / 90 mm) and both rail orientations (slide on from the side or front/back) are bundled. Japandi fluted side faces to match the Moku look. A remix of the Moku Base Rail by @SabreDesign / @Collecticraft.
