Marsh — Backlit Modular LED Swamp Terrain Runner (28-32mm, chainable)

A backlit modular swamp runner for 28-32mm tabletop games. A winding translucent creek glows from below when a 30cm USB LED strip slides into a hidden channel. Two printed parts per segment (one is a two-colour terrain plate), no supports, no glue. Identical segments dovetail end-to-end so one strip threads straight through the whole run; the river, banks and bog texture flow continuously across every joint. Confirmed printed and lit.

Parametric Stackable Storage Box (1×1 to 5×5)

Boxes & storage / Organizers / Baskets

Stackable Lattice Basket — 4 Sizes + Parametric SCAD

Cross-stackable lattice baskets with cone-peg corner posts — any basket stacks onto any other basket. Four ready-to-print sizes from 5×5×5 cm cube up to a 20×12×8 cm tray, plus an optional cone-foot leg accessory. One open short side per basket so you can reach contents while the stack stays put. Parametric SCAD included — override basket_l/basket_w/body_h to derive any size; corner-post + peg geometry is shared so derived sizes still cross-stack with the printed ones.

Stackable Server Rack — Tool-Free Mini-PC / NUC / SBC Shelf

A modular, tool-free rack for mini-PCs, NUCs, SBCs and other small servers. Each level is a honeycomb-vented floor on four fat corner posts; levels self-stack on cone pegs (no screws, no hardware) and four wide feet lift the whole tower off the desk for airflow. Print one floor per device, four feet once, stack as tall as you like. Parametric SCAD — resize for any box.

Desk & office / Organizers / Modular

Moku Iris Box — Working Camera-Aperture Catch-All (Base Rail module)

A round fluted catch-all box for the Moku modular desk system whose lid is a WORKING 6-blade iris diaphragm — twist the knurled collar and the blades spiral open like a camera aperture onto the cavity (Ø48 mm) for clips, SD cards and coins. Same-plane pinwheel iris: 6 identical rigid blades on pivot posts, drive pins riding radial collar slots, snap-on collar, 1-minute no-order assembly, zero supports. Clips onto the host Base Rail by the reverse-measured foot interface. A remix of the Moku Base Rail by @SabreDesign / @Collecticraft.

Moku Memo Stand — Sloped Sticky-Note Writing Tray (Base Rail module, 3 sizes)

A sloped sticky-note writing tray that clips onto the Moku modular desk-system Base Rail. The pad sits on a 21° incline so the top sheet faces you — read it and write on it in place. Low front rim plus a centre finger-scoop to peel the top sheet; tall back and side rims keep the pad square. Three pad sizes (60 / 80 / 90 mm) and both rail orientations (slide on from the side or front/back) are bundled. Japandi fluted side faces to match the Moku look. Jewelry & accessories / Jewelry displays

Moku Cantilever Watch Stand — open 'C' bracket + parametric engraved clock (Base Rail module)

A cantilever 'C' watch stand for a chunky sport/dive watch that clips onto the Moku modular desk-system Base Rail. A soft, near-rectangular bracket with smoothly filleted joints: a back spine carries a top arm that cantilevers forward over an open gap, its rest face CANTED ~20° SIDE-TO-SIDE so the watch leans on a dynamic diagonal. The watch sits centred over the base (so a heavy steel watch won't tip it off the rail); the bracelet drapes over the rounded front nose and hangs free, its end on the front shelf. The tall SPINE FRONT face — the back wall of the C, in full view through the opening — carries an ENGRAVED CLOCK FACE with PARAMETRIC hands (set the time in the SCAD, default 10:10) ringed by gear teeth. Every edge is rounded (Japandi). Prints PRINT-ON-END so the cantilever needs no supports and the rail foot prints as crisp vertical channels. A remix of the Moku Base Rail by @SabreDesign / @Collecticraft.
