Board games / Find your game / Magic: The Gathering / Deck boxes & card care

Mana Press — screw press for Magic cards: flatten a bowed deck, brick a freshly sleeved one

A printed screw press for Magic cards. Two threaded posts, a yoke and two thumb nuts squeeze a stack flat overnight, or squeeze a freshly sleeved deck back into a brick that drops into its box. The posts ARE the screws — there is no hardware anywhere in the model, no screws, magnets or inserts. Height is set by stacking 12 mm pressure plates, which double as the WUBRG mana rainbow and each ship a named-solid 2-colour companion for AMS. An optional, experimental arc pair over-bends a curled foil past its set. 11 STLs, 6 printed parts, no supports, fully parametric OpenSCAD.

Boxes & storage / Keepsake boxes / Nature

Rut Box — a keepsake box locked shut by two stags' antlers

A 108 x 62 x 84 mm hunting keepsake box with a 64 ml cavity. Four antler racks — two on the base, two on the lid — interleave when the box is closed, like two stags with their horns locked in the rut. A quarter turn sweeps the racks apart and releases a bayonet lock. No hardware, no supports, no glue.

Around the home / Hooks

Bag Hook for a Desk Edge or a Chair Back

Clamps onto a table edge, a desk lip or a chair backrest and gets the backpack off the floor. One part, no supports, no screws, no glue. Three sizes included — 20, 25 and 30 mm jaws — and a sprung tongue inside each takes up the slack below that, so between them they cover roughly 10 to 30 mm. A 7-minute fit-test coupon is included for every size so you can check the grip on your own table before printing the whole thing. The source file is included and fully parametric: change one number and you get a jaw for any thickness.
