Board games / Classic games / Dominoes

Domino Box — Sleeve + Tray for the Double-Six 28-Tile Set (OpenSCAD)

Two-piece storage for a full 28-tile double-six domino set. Outer sleeve (closed on 5 faces, open on one short end) + inner tray with a Ø28 mm semicircular thumb-notch on its front wall. Tiles lie flat in a 4×2 grid of 4-tile stacks (32-slot capacity). Sleeve prints with the open mouth UP — fully supportless. Engraved decorative pip pattern on every long face. Parametric SCAD lets you re-tune tile dimensions, slop, layout (piles_x × piles_y), pip pattern, or wall thicknesses.

Boxes & storage / Organizers / Baskets

Stackable Lattice Basket — 4 Sizes + Parametric SCAD

Cross-stackable lattice baskets with cone-peg corner posts — any basket stacks onto any other basket. Four ready-to-print sizes from 5×5×5 cm cube up to a 20×12×8 cm tray, plus an optional cone-foot leg accessory. One open short side per basket so you can reach contents while the stack stays put. Parametric SCAD included — override basket_l/basket_w/body_h to derive any size; corner-post + peg geometry is shared so derived sizes still cross-stack with the printed ones.

Stacking Letter / A4 Paper Tray — modular, honeycomb floor, 20 swappable nameplates

A classic 4-post stacking in-out tray for A4 or Letter paper, sized to fit a 256 mm bed. Paper sits landscape and overhangs the low open front so you can grab a sheet one-handed. Stack as many tiers as you print — corner pegs drop into the tier below. Comes with a pack of 20 clip-on nameplates (IN · OUT · TODO · URGENT · BILLS · CHAOS …) plus a blank one to write on. No screws, no glue, no supports.

MagDock — Magnetic Desk & Shop Organizer Family

A family of small organizers that embed neodymium discs in their backs, so they snap onto ANY ferrous surface — printer frame, fridge, toolbox, filing cabinet, radiator. Hook, tray, tall caddy, pliers/scissors slots, and a bare puck. The magnets are sealed inside DURING printing (mid-print pause), so no caps and no glue. Every part prints flat on its back with NO supports.

Desk & office / Organizers / Paper Notes

Moku Memo Stand — Sloped Sticky-Note Writing Tray (Base Rail module, 3 sizes)

A sloped sticky-note writing tray that clips onto the Moku modular desk-system Base Rail. The pad sits on a 21° incline so the top sheet faces you — read it and write on it in place. Low front rim plus a centre finger-scoop to peel the top sheet; tall back and side rims keep the pad square. Three pad sizes (60 / 80 / 90 mm) and both rail orientations (slide on from the side or front/back) are bundled. Japandi fluted side faces to match the Moku look. A remix of the Moku Base Rail by @SabreDesign / @Collecticraft.

Sink-Rim Sponge Tray — Hooks Over the Rim, Drains Into the Basin

Single-bay sponge tray that hooks over a kitchen sink rim and hangs inside the basin. Sized to a standard kitchen sponge (~95×60×25 mm) with ~5 mm slack on every side. Floor drain slots + lower-wall drains let water run straight back into the sink — sponge dries instead of sitting in a puddle. Prints in one piece, open-top up, no supports.
