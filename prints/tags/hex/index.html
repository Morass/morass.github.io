MTG — Mana Barrel Counter (3-Drum Hex Dial)

Three-digit (0-999) rotating barrel counter for Magic: the Gathering — hex prism with three independently-rotating drums, capped at each end by a hex cap engraved with the chosen mana symbol (W/U/B/R/G or colourless).
