Board games / Dice

Crit Dice — d6 / d12 / d20 whose top face is a sword, bow or staff

Nine dice: three solids x three glyphs. Every ordinary face is a plain numeral you read at a glance; only the crit face is a picture, so a natural 20 lands as a drawn sword instead of a number. Fair on all nine (opposite faces sum to 7 / 13 / 21). Single-colour, or two-colour from one file on an AMS.

Decorations / Magnets

Wizard Figurine — robed mage with a magnet hand

A robed wizard figurine with a pointed wide-brim hat, long beard and one hand raised in greeting. The raised hand has a recessed slot for a small disc magnet, so he can hold a metal wand, pin or trinket. It is NOT a fridge magnet — the figure will not stick to a fridge, and the little magnet is weak, so think of it as a charming desk or shelf wizard with a handy magnetic hand. Ships as an STL plus a ready-to-slice Bambu .3mf.
