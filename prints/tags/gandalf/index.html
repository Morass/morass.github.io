Wizard Figurine — robed mage with a magnet hand

A robed wizard figurine with a pointed wide-brim hat, long beard and one hand raised in greeting. It is NOT a fridge magnet — the figure will not stick to a fridge, and the little magnet is weak, so think of it as a charming desk or shelf wizard with a handy magnetic hand.
