Library of Alexandria box — a magnetic box under the great dome

The Library of Alexandria as a box: a white-marble hall behind an arcade of pointed arches, a full-width stair, four turrets under gilded onion domes and a great ribbed dome on a chevroned drum. Capybara Lock Box — Magnetic Two-Key Drawer Box

A drawer box that only opens when you set TWO capybara figurines on its lid pads. The capybaras are the keys — each hides a magnet that lifts a hidden loose-magnet shear pin off the drawer rail. Both keys at once = a two-capybara combination lock. Multi-color engraved lid, captive drawer, self-locking. Uses hobby disc magnets you drop in during printing. Keys included.

Jewelry & accessories / Brooches

Half-Amphora Brooch — a real amphora, full of flowers, cut in half

A magnetic brooch, 44 x 58 mm. Not an illusion and not a flat silhouette: a proper amphora is turned as a body of revolution, given two handles and a spray of flowers, and then cut in half on the plane of the brooch's back. What is left is 6 mm of true 3D relief with a flat back. The halving is also the print trick — a solid of revolution cut on its axis plane has zero overhang anywhere, so it prints face up with no supports. Two printed pieces, four colours, sealed magnets, no pin.

Hollow-Face Vase Brooch — the vase is a hole, and it follows you

A magnetic brooch, 47 x 55 mm. The vase is not raised — it is the exact mould of a vase, sunk 5.2 mm into the plaque. Your eye refuses to read it as hollow, so it bulges out instead, and appears to turn and follow whoever walks past. Nothing moves; the viewer does. Two printed pieces, four colours in one 0.6 mm band, sealed magnets, no pin.

Decorations / Magnets

Weather Dial Fridge Magnet — spin it to today's weather

A fridge magnet with a live front. Thumb the knurled rim through the side notch and the dial clicks into four detented positions — sun, rain, cloud, snow — showing one icon at a time through the porthole. The three magnets are sealed inside solid plastic on both faces: no glue, no exposed metal against the door, nothing to fall out. They go in during the print, at a single pause. The dial ships as five files sharing one coordinate frame, so it prints in five colours with nothing to group by hand. Prints supportless. Parametric OpenSCAD source included.

Wizard Figurine — robed mage with a magnet hand

A robed wizard figurine with a pointed wide-brim hat, long beard and one hand raised in greeting. The raised hand has a recessed slot for a small disc magnet, so he can hold a metal wand, pin or trinket. It is NOT a fridge magnet — the figure will not stick to a fridge, and the little magnet is weak, so think of it as a charming desk or shelf wizard with a handy magnetic hand.
