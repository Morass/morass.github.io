Stackable Server Rack — Tool-Free Mini-PC / NUC / SBC Shelf

A modular, tool-free rack for mini-PCs, NUCs, SBCs and other small servers. Each level is a honeycomb-vented floor on four fat corner posts; levels self-stack on cone pegs (no screws, no hardware) and four wide feet lift the whole tower off the desk for airflow. Print one floor per device, four feet once, stack as tall as you like. Parametric SCAD — resize for any box.

Desk & office / Electronics / Stands

Keyboard Riser — Split, Dovetail-Joined, Parametric (60 / 100 / 180 mm Lift)

Open-frame stand that lifts a keyboard off the desk, leaving the space underneath clear. Three ready-to-print configurations: 60 and 100 mm lift for a 440 × 135 mm board, and 180 mm for a 313 × 123 mm 75 % board (Keychron K2 size). A 3 mm rim keeps the board from sliding off; a honeycomb deck spreads its weight. Too long for a 256 mm bed, so it splits into two halves that re-join with a drop-in dovetail key — no glue, no tools. Fully parametric: change one variable for any other board or height.

Stacking Letter / A4 Paper Tray — modular, honeycomb floor, 20 swappable nameplates

A classic 4-post stacking in-out tray for A4 or Letter paper, sized to fit a 256 mm bed. Paper sits landscape and overhangs the low open front so you can grab a sheet one-handed. Stack as many tiers as you print — corner pegs drop into the tier below. Comes with a pack of 20 clip-on nameplates (IN · OUT · TODO · URGENT · BILLS · CHAOS …) plus a blank one to write on. No screws, no glue, no supports.
