Kitchen / Tableware

Wave-Crest Napkin Ring — sculpted, no supports

A napkin ring whose top rim rises and falls in three slow waves, with the wall swelling thickest directly under each crest so the band resolves into three lobes with a waist between them. The bore stays a true cylinder, so a rolled cloth napkin always passes. Every curved surface faces up or outward and the bottom rim is flat on the bed, which means zero overhang by construction: it prints standing, exactly as modelled, with no supports and no brim.
