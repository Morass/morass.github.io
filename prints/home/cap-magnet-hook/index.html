Cap Magnet Hook

A little magnetic hook shaped like a baseball cap — the curved brim is the hook. Two Ø10x5 mm neodymium discs sit sealed inside the flat back and stick it to any steel surface (fridge, locker, radiator, steel door frame). No screws, no supports. A screw-mount version is included too. Holds LIGHT things only.
