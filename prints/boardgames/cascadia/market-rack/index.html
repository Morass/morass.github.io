Cascadia Market Rack + 3D Wildlife Tokens

A two-in-one Cascadia upgrade: a market-row display rack that holds the four face-up habitat tile + wildlife token pairs on a gentle 15° slope, PLUS a full set of five sculpted 3D wildlife tokens (bear, elk, salmon, hawk, fox) that replace the flat wooden discs and drop straight into the rack's Ø19 cups. The rack prints flat and supportless in one part; each animal prints upright on a Ø18 base disc in its own colour — no AMS, no paint. Parametric OpenSCAD source included.
