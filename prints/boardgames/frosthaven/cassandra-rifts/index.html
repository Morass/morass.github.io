Cassandra Rift Overlay Hex Tokens — Frosthaven / Gloomhaven (3-colour, a mini stands on it)

3D rift overlay hex tokens for Cassandra the Forgotten Diviner (Frosthaven / Gloomhaven). Each is a 38.1 mm flat-top map hex with a recessed flat central plaza a mini can stand on, ringed by a raised ornate runic frame. Three-colour AMS print: dark stone body, a magenta energy vortex flush in the plaza floor, and gold raised runes on the frame. Prints support-free — every feature steps up. Includes a fit-test coupon and the parametric OpenSCAD source.
