Travel Cribbage Peg Track — Captive Slide-In Pegs, 1 to 6 Players

A pocket cribbage scorer with captive scoring pegs that can never fall out. Each player's peg rides a hidden T-channel and clicks into every score station on a self-centring ball-detent, so it always lands exactly on the number. Slide the pegs into the open end, snap the end cap on, and they are enclosed for good. One parametric design, bundled in every player count from 1 to 6.
