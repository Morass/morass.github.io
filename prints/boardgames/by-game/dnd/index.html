Adventurer Dial Base — a mini base that counts your damage 0–9

A Ø36 mm miniature base with a rotating 0–9 counter ring built into it. It prints in place: the disc and the ring come off the plate already interlocked, no assembly, no hardware. A hooded wanderer figure is included — drop it in, or print the version with it welded on.

D&D Death Save Tracker — slide up for a heart, down for a skull

A flat, silent death-saving-throw tracker for D&D 5e. Three captive sliders: push one UP and a red heart fills the top window (success), push it DOWN and a white skull fills the bottom one (failure), leave it centred and both stay blank. Three hearts you're stable, three skulls you're dead — the whole table can read your state from across it. Five printed parts, no glue, no screws, no dice-sized bits to lose; everything prints flat and supportless. Parametric OpenSCAD source included.

Spell-Slot Abacus — print-in-place D&D spell-slot tracker

A print-in-place abacus for tracking D&D spell slots: 9 rails (spell levels 1–9) of captive sliding crystal beads (4/3/3/3/3/2/2/1/1 = full-caster max). Slide a bead to burn a slot, sweep back on a long rest. A tilt-lock detent at mid-span keeps the count from scrambling when you pick the board up. ~107 × 130 × 8 mm, zero supports.
