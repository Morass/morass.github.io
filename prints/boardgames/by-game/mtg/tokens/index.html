Highly detailed citadel emblem for Magic: The Gathering's City's Blessing Ascend mechanic. Print in metallic gold (the original was Bambu metallic PLA) and seat on any small disc base to track when you have 10+ permanents.

MTG — Day / Night Flip Token (60mm Disc)

60 mm two-sided flip token for the Day/Night mechanic in Magic: the Gathering. Sun (with 12 radiating rays) on the day face; crescent moon + 3 stars on the night face. Flip the token whenever a card transitions Day↔Night so the current state is always visible. Two-colour AMS print: dark body + gold engravings.

MTG — Food · Blood · Clue Artifact Token Set

A set of three 3D artifact tokens for Magic: the Gathering, replacing the cardboard punch-outs: FOOD as a realistic lattice pie, BLOOD as a faceted gem-cut drop, and CLUE as a detective's magnifying glass. Each token prints flat, supportless, in one colour (or paint the details). ~22–30 mm, PLA. Parametric OpenSCAD source included.

Marit Lage 20/20 Token — MTG flat-card display plinth with eldritch sculpt + multicolour text

A display plinth for the Marit Lage token from Magic: The Gathering (Dark Depths) — laid out like a Magic card lying flat (≈90 × 60 mm, low 4 mm base). A colossal Lovecraftian tentacle sculpt sits as the 'art', RAISED multicolour text reads MARIT LAGE on top, INDESTRUCTIBLE / FLYING in front of the art, and a big 20/20 power/toughness on the front vertical wall. Prints flat, plinth-on-bed, creature up — supportless. Ships as ONE combined STL with named solids for Bambu 'Split to Parts': one per BLOCK (combined) or one per CHARACTER (chars) for clean per-letter AMS colouring with zero accent bleed.

Monarch Crown Token (MTG) — 3-Colour Heraldic Crown, OpenSCAD

Heraldic crown token for the Monarch mechanic in Magic: The Gathering. Slow-cylinder bell band + 7 tall triangular spikes alternating with 7 short crenel spikes, Ø5 mm orb finials on each tall tip. "MONARCH" wraps around the band as inset 2-colour text. 3-colour print via Bambu AMS — combined named-solid STL (Split-to-Parts) ships alongside per-filament STLs.

MTG — Pact Reminder Token (YOU WILL PAY Scroll)

Medieval-scroll reminder token for Magic: the Gathering. Drop in front of an opponent who owes you a Pact upkeep (Pact of Negation, Slaughter Pact, Intervention Pact, Pact of the Titans, Summoner's Pact) or anyone trying to skip a delayed cost. 55×80 mm slim slab with raised parchment-roll ridges top + bottom and 'YOU WILL PAY' in raised medieval lettering. Two-colour AMS print: parchment body + ink letters.

MTG Powerstone Token — Flip-to-Tap Crystal

Chunky 60 × 30 × 16 mm faceted crystal token for Magic: the Gathering's Powerstone artifacts. It rests flat on either octagonal face: plain side up is untapped, tap-arrow side up is tapped. Flip it instead of rotating it. Prints as one part with no supports.
