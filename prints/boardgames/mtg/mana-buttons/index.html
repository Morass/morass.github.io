MTG Mana Buttons — all six mana symbols as real sewing buttons, 15 / 20 / 25 mm, hidden shank or 2-hole

Wearable sewing buttons carrying all six Magic mana symbols — white sun, blue drop, black skull, red fireball, green tree and the colorless lozenge. 30 STLs: 6 symbols x 5 size/style combos. The 'tunnel' style hides a thread shank in the underside so the face stays whole and nothing is bored through the emblem; the 'holed' style is a classic 2-hole shirt button with both bores in the plain ring. Ø15 / 20 / 25 mm (24L / 32L / 40L). Every button ships a named-solid 2-colour companion for AMS. Prints flat, face up, no supports, no brim, no hardware. Fully parametric OpenSCAD — any diameter, and the glyph strokes are held at or above one nozzle width automatically.
