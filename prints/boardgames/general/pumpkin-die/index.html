Pumpkin d6 — Every Pip Is a Pumpkin, the 1 Is a Jack-o'-Lantern

A standard 20 mm six-sided die where all 21 pips are little pumpkins and the lone 1 is a carved jack-o'-lantern. Prints in two colours from a single STL — import, Split to Parts, done. No supports, no brim.
