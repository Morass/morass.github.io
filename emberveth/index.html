Emberveth

Get it on Steam Join the Community

Read the boss. Don't burn your friends.

Emberveth is a couch co-op spellcaster for one to four players, set in the world of Orenvalt. Aim every spell by hand and jump what you cannot walk around.

Fifteen bespoke bosses ask you to read their mechanics and respond. Friendly fire is always on, so your ally's fireball hurts.

What's in the Box

One to Four Players

Local couch co-op for one to four players.

Aim by Hand

Every spell is aimed manually, and you can jump what you cannot walk around.

Fifteen Bespoke Bosses

Fights you have to read and respond to.

Friendly Fire Always On

Your ally's fireball hurts. Plan around each other.
