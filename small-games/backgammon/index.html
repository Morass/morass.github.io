The dice you can check

Full backgammon: hitting, the bar, bearing off, gammons and backgammons, and matches of 1, 3, 5 or 7 points with the doubling cube and the Crawford rule. Before the first roll the table shows a seal that fixes every die of the match; you may cut it with words of your own, and when the match ends the secret is revealed so anyone can recompute every roll. The ledger counts both sides' luck too.

Seven computer keepers, from Deckhand to Pilot, share one judgement trained by self-play; each step up the ladder was measured over a thousand games on shared dice. After each keeper play, Why? shows the plays it liked and its own estimates. The 120 Trials are bear-offs where one play wins most often, worked out exactly; the fastest first solve of each goes on the board.
