Warding Towers

Comets are falling on the city. Burn them out of the sky.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

Three towers, six districts, one night

The arcade game Missile Command, on the wardens' wall of a fantasy city. Comets rain down on six districts of lit timber houses. Tap the sky just ahead of a comet: the nearest tower throws a ward-bolt there, and it bursts into a rune circle that burns every comet inside it. A burning comet bursts into a smaller ward of its own, so one good throw can chain through a cluster.

Each wave is faster and thicker, with splitting comets and wyverns that drop more; each tower has ten bolts a wave, and what you save scores at the tally. The night ends when the last district burns. The endless night is a new sky every time and the daily night is the same sky for everyone; the Ten keep the best of each, and every night on them was re-played by the site before it was ranked.
