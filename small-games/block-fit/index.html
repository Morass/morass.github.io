HOW TO PLAY

Mend the broken ward.

  1. Drag a shard onto the tablet — the outline shows the slot it will drop into.
  2. Fill every square with no gaps and no overlaps. Shards never turn.

Forty-eight tablets across four sizes plus a daily. Every board is proved to fit together exactly one way, and each keeps a public best time.
