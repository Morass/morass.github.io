Six sea-witches and 120 Hunts

Battleships with Salt-Wyrms instead of ships. Hide your five wyrms in your fogbank, then take turns casting into your rival's: a splash is water, a strike lights a coil, and the last strike on a wyrm makes it surface. Wyrms never touch, not even at a corner, so the water around a surfaced wyrm clears. The rival's brood is sealed before your first cast, and the seal opens at the end so you can check it never moved.

Six sea-witches, from the forgetful Gull-Witch to the Storm-Caller, were each measured a step stronger than the last; after any of their casts, Why? shows how they chose. The 120 Hunts give you the fishers' soundings, the wyrm squares under every row and column, and each has exactly one answer found without a guess. Each Hunt keeps the fastest first solve and the fewest casts.
