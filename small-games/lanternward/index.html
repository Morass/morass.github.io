Lanternward

Light the night streets of Veldrenmar. 300 graded Akari puzzles and a new one every day, each with a single solution you can reason out.

Play now ↗ Play on CrazyGames ↗

Free to play · No account · Plays on morassgames.com and CrazyGames; Y8, GamePix and Playgama follow once their reviews finish

HOW TO PLAY

Light every street.

  1. Click a street cell to place a lantern. It lights its row and column until a roof stops it.
  2. Numbered roofs say exactly how many lanterns stand beside them.
  3. No lantern may shine on another. When every street is lit, the district brightens.

Click a lantern again to mark a dot (no lantern here), once more to clear it; hold the button to mark a dot directly. Hint and Show my mistake each cost one star. Your progress is saved in the browser you play in. The same game is free on Android and iOS with all sixteen languages; the browser edition is in English.

Mouse or touch. The stopwatch is a challenge, never a countdown.
