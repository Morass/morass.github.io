Traffic Jam

Roll the carts aside. Get the red wagon out of the gate.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

180 yards, every one proved

The sliding-block puzzle best known as Rush Hour. Every cart rolls only along its length, left and right or up and down, and the stone wells never move. Roll carts out of the way until the Caravan-Master's red wagon has a clear road, then roll it out through the gate in the east wall. One cart rolled any distance is one move.

The yards come from Michael Fogleman's complete study of the puzzle, and each is the position farthest from solved in its whole family, so none is a shortcut into an easier one. The game proves every yard's fewest moves with its own search and shows it before you start: six yards of thirty from the Stable Yard (4 to 8 moves) to the Caravan-Master's Yard (40 to 60), and a daily caravan that is the same for everyone. Each yard keeps two public records with their holders' names: fewest moves, which a replay can still improve, and fastest first solve.
