Snake

Gather the motes. The floor you have crossed is the only thing that can stop you.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

HOW TO PLAY

Grow without crossing yourself.

  1. Arrows, WASD, a swipe or the four ward-stones turn the serpent; it never stops on its own.
  2. Every mote makes it one segment longer and a little quicker.
  3. Sealed walls end the run. Seamed walls carry the light out of the opposite side.

Three chamber sizes — 11×11, 17×17 and 23×23 — each played sealed or seamed, and each of the six keeps its own board of the ten best runs. The record is the number of motes, and a tie keeps the older holder.
