2048

Slide the forge. Join equal embers. Wake the Phoenix.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

HOW TO PLAY

Forge 2048.

  1. Swipe, press an arrow or WASD, or tap a direction plate to slide every tile together.
  2. Equal values fuse once during a move, and every accepted move raises one new ember.
  3. Climb from Ember through Flame, Beacon and Starfire to forge the Phoenix at 2048 — then keep going if you wish.

The exact classic four-by-four rules, rebuilt as a physical ember forge with carved sigils, moving tiles, impact sounds and a personal best that stays in this browser. No timer and no public board.
