Mancala

Every rule shown on the board before you need it.

Learn it by sowing

Kalah, the Mancala most people know: six pits a side, four seeds in each. Sow from your row counter-clockwise, into your own granary and past your opponent's. A last seed in your granary sows again; a last seed in an empty pit of yours captures the seeds facing it; when a row runs dry the fuller granary wins. Tap a pit and every pit its seeds will reach is numbered, with what the last seed will do, before you commit.

Five short lessons teach each rule on the real board. Six tithe-factors, from a careless gleaner-boy to the Tithe-Master, were each measured stronger than the last over three hundred games, and Why? shows how they scored every pit. The 120 Trials are endgames where exactly one first sowing keeps a win against a defender that never errs; each keeps the fastest first win and the fewest sowings.
