Word Hunt

Trace words through touching stones before the sand runs out.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

Fifteen maps, 450 boards

The letter-grid word game, on the survey sheets of a fantasy map-makers' commission. Each map lays its letter-stones out in the shape of a place: a square well, a long bridge, a crossroads, a ring around a hole, a crown, a star. Stones touch side by side and corner to corner, holes break the touch, and each stone counts once per word. Drag, tap or type; longer words score far more, and when the hourglass runs out the sheet shows every common word you missed.

Three difficulties: Apprentice maps take words of three letters, Journeyman four and Master five. Every map has thirty boards from generous to sparse, and a Wander board that is new every time; three daily hunts are the same for everyone. The Ten keep the best scores of each board, and the site re-traces every word of a hunt before it is ranked.
