Pyramid Solitaire

Pair to thirteen. Unbind the pyramid.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

HOW TO PLAY

Clear the Moon Archivist’s table.

  1. Select two exposed cards whose ranks total thirteen: Ace and Queen, Two and Jack, through Six and Seven.
  2. An exposed King clears alone. Draw one card at a time when the pyramid has no useful pair; only the top waste card is available.
  3. Clear all twenty-eight pyramid cards to win. Undo or restart whenever the route closes.

One hundred and twenty full-deck deals are open from the beginning. Every deal was built from a legal clear and its complete route is replayed through the shipping rules before release. Each pyramid keeps named public records for fastest time and fewest moves.
