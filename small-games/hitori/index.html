Raise the Causeway on the Mirelight Mere

Two stones carved with the same number in one row or column echo. Sink stones until no number stands twice, never two sunk stones side by side, and keep every standing stone joined in one unbroken causeway.

All 120 crossings are open across six reaches, from 6×6 shallows to 11×11 deep water. Every one has exactly one answer, reached by reasoning with no guess, and keeps a named public fastest time.
