SMALL GAMES / CARD GAME

FreeCell

Every card face up. Four cells for what will not fit yet.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

HOW TO PLAY

Send every suit home, ace to king.

  1. Columns pack downward in alternating colours, and an empty column takes any card.
  2. The four cells each hold one card — deciding what goes in them is the whole game.
  3. An ordered run travels as one, as far as the free cells and empty columns can carry it.

A thousand of the real numbered deals, so deal 617 here is deal 617 anywhere else — and every one of them solved before it shipped. Four difficulty tiers, a new deal each day, and a best time and fewest moves kept on every deal.
