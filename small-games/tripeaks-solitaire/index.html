TriPeaks Solitaire

One higher or one lower. Clear all three peaks.

Three peaks, a thousand deals

TriPeaks Solitaire at a shrine-station lodge high in the mountains. Twenty-eight cards make three peaks, most of them face down. Play any open card one rank above or below the waste card, any suit, and King and Ace touch; a face-down card turns over when the two cards on it are gone. Chain a long run, choose which card to take when two will play, and clear the peaks before the one-pass stock runs out.

There are 1,000 numbered deals in four ranges, Foothills to Summits, measured by how often a careful player who cannot see the hidden cards wins each one, and a Day's Deal that is the same for everyone. Every deal is proved winnable, and when you are stuck the game plays the winning line on the real cards. Each deal keeps a named best time and fewest moves.
