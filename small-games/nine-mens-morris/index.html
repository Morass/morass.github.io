Nine Men's Morris

Place nine stones. Close a mill. Break the rival line.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

Bind the ward-table

Place nine stones on the twenty-four crossings. A straight board line of three closes a mill and removes a rival stone. After placement, slide along the brass paths; with only three stones left, fly to any open crossing.

Face Kindler, Wayfinder, Gate Warden or Oathkeeper, or share the board with a nearby friend. The 120 always-open Mill Trials each come from a legal game and have exactly one move that closes a mill. Each keeps a named public fastest first-solve time; keeper wins remember your fewest turns and best time.
