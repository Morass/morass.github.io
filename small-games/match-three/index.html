Match Three

The chutes show what falls next, so a cascade is something you build.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

HOW TO PLAY

Line up three of a kind.

  1. Tap a stone and then its neighbour, or drag one at the other, to swap them.
  2. Three or more in a row or a column flare and go; the column falls and the chutes feed it.
  3. Read the chutes — the stone you can see coming is the one that finishes the line you are about to open.

Four in a line leaves a Burst, an L or a T leaves a Star, and five leaves a Prism that takes a whole colour off the bench. Four ways to play: Endless (with three free shuffles when the bench runs out of swaps), a sixty-second run, thirty swaps with no clock, and one daily bench everybody shares. Each keeps the ten best runs with the name of whoever set them, and every one of those ten is a replay you can watch.
