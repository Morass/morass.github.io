Solitaire

A warden's Klondike table, dealt fairly and proved finishable.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

HOW TO PLAY

Set every suit home.

  1. Build the seven columns downward, alternating red and black.
  2. Turn the stock and send each suit home from ace through king.
  3. Open every covered card; an empty column takes a king.

Choose Draw 1 or Draw 3 across 120 shuffled tables. Every deal is solver-proved from its opening layout. Undo is unlimited, and a hint is always close at hand.
