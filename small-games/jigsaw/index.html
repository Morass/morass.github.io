Jigsaw

Set the shards of a broken pane back into the lead. Drop one near its place and it takes.

Play now ↗

Free · No account · Plays on morassgames.com

HOW TO PLAY

Make the pane whole again.

  1. Pick a picture and a cut, and drag a shard towards where it belongs.
  2. Near is near enough: let it go anywhere close and it takes, with a click, and nothing you do afterwards can knock it out.
  3. A shard dropped anywhere else stays exactly where you put it, so you can sort the bench into colours and it will keep.

Twelve painted places at four leaded cuts — six shards to ninety-six — and a fresh scramble and a fresh cut every time. No rotation, no zoom and nothing too small to grab; no advertisement between you and the piece you were placing. Leave, reload, or lose the tab and the pane is waiting with every shard where you left it. Forty-eight public boards of ten, and nothing sends a number: a finished pane is sent as its replay and set again on the server.
