A keeper for every step

Full chess rules: castling, en passant, promotion, and draws by stalemate, repetition, fifty moves and too little material. Play one of ten computer keepers, from Page, who is just learning the moves, to Oracle, the strongest this table holds. Every step up the ladder was measured in a tournament to beat the one below clearly, never by a cliff. Or share the board with a friend.

The Trials are 200 checkmates in one to five moves and 20 endgame drills with king and queen or king and rook. Every answer was proved by a solver. The clock starts when a Trial is dealt; the fastest first solve on each Trial goes on the board.
