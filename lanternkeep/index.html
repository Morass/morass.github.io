Lanternkeep

The table helper that keeps your game night running, so you can keep playing.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

About

Lanternkeep is a free, offline table helper for tabletop role-playing and board-game nights. There is no account, it works without a connection and there is nothing to buy. One small banner ad appears in Settings.

  • Initiative order with HP, conditions and concentration
  • Torch and round timers
  • 3D dice roller
  • Character sheet
  • Notes
  • Rules reference built on the System Reference Document 5.2.1
