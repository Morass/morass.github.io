About the Game

Light every street. A logic puzzle in the night streets of Veldrenmar: a lantern lights its row and column until a rooftop stops it, numbered roofs say how many lanterns stand beside them, and no lantern may shine on another.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, solvable by reasoning alone.

What's in the Box

10 Districts + Endless Night

Work through ten hand-built districts of Veldrenmar, then keep going in Endless Night once you've lit them all.

Daily Puzzle

One fresh puzzle every day, the same for everyone — come back and compare notes.

Four Stars by Time

Every level scores up to four stars by how fast you solve it. Pure reasoning, no luck.

Optional Hints

Stuck? A short ad unlocks a hint. Never required — the puzzle is always solvable without one.

Offline

No connection needed to play. Lanternward runs entirely on your device.

16 Languages

Fully localised into sixteen languages, with a colour-blind-friendly palette built in.
