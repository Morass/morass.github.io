About the Game

Pyrewarden is a fantasy tower defense built around emergent synergy. Twelve magic schools, 36 distinct upgrade paths with deep per-slot customisation, and 66 hand-tuned synergy combos that fire when adjacent towers strike the same enemy.

There is no end — only the next wave, the next pairing, the next combo nobody's discovered yet.

What's in the Box

12 Schools

Fire, Frost, Lightning, Poison, Earth, Shadow, Light, Arcane, Air, Nature, Time, Beasts — each with a counter-web of ±30% damage modifiers.

36 Upgrade Paths

3 paths per school, each with 10 customisable slots. Every path blends three of six gameplay roles: DPS, AoE, control, support, debuff, DoT.

66 Synergies

Every school pair has its own combo. Place Fire next to Frost for Thermal Flux. Land both schools' damage to detonate Shatterstorm.

26 Maps

Hand-built maps with varied route layouts, dynamic pathing, and per-map element bias.

Endless Waves

Milestone waves every 10 ramp difficulty. Enemies gain a new ability every 5 waves from wave 10 — armor, stealth, regen, healing, magic shield, spawn-on-death.

1–6 Player Co-op

Quick-join public rooms or share a 6-character room code over Steam matchmaking.
